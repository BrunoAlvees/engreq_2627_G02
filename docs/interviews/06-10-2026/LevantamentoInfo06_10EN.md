# FAIR-AMAP - Information gathered in the 06/10 session

**Updated on:** 07/10/2026

This document gathers the information collected in the 06/10 session, held during the theoretical class, with the participation of Luisa Marques, an active co-producer of AMAP UPTEC Baixa.

The information was gathered by the group members during the session. Points the group is not sure about are marked "to be confirmed". The information gathered here represents the group's current understanding and does not yet constitute a specification validated by the client.

## 1. Interviewed stakeholder

Luisa Marques is an active co-producer of AMAP UPTEC Baixa and is usually involved in its management groups. Luisa Marques also takes part in the AMAP's assembly.

After the 29/09 session, which presented a producer's point of view (BioGoods), this session brought the point of view of a **co-producer** involved in the AMAP's management.

## 2. Document analysed: order form

The stakeholder showed the **order form** used in the AMAP, a Google Form, for the July to December period. From what was shown:

- The form brings together several producers. The first one is BioGoods.
- Some products are ordered separately, for example **eggs**.
- Some products have several options. For example, there are **two types of mushrooms** and both can be selected.
- The form includes the **contacts of the various AMAP**.

After submitting the form, the co-producer usually receives **a copy of the answers by email**. This copy was described as useful, because orders are placed well in advance and people do not always remember what they ordered.

## 3. Operation described in the session

### 3.1. Several producers in the same AMAP

Several producers take part in AMAP UPTEC Baixa, each with its own payment and delivery rules. The following were mentioned:

- **BioGoods** (Sofia Araújo), the main producer.
- **Quinta da Serrinha** (Daniel), which supplies olive oil.

### 3.2. Payments

The payment method **depends on each producer**:

- **BioGoods:** sends the co-producer the amount calculated from what was **actually delivered**, because products are sometimes missing (for example, eggs).
- **Quinta da Serrinha:** prefers payment to be made **before** the order is placed or delivered, because the amounts involved are larger.

The stakeholder pays by **MB Way**, but **bank transfer** is also possible.

### 3.3. Deliveries

**Delivery dates are set by the producers.**

In the case of Quinta da Serrinha, the producer contacts co-producers **by email and by message**, because olive oil has a higher value, and **only delivers in person**.

### 3.4. Absences

It was mentioned that, when a co-producer cannot receive the order (for example, during holidays), they can give it to other people. This is consistent with what was described in the 29/09 session. The exact rule about holidays was not clear (to be confirmed).

### 3.5. Damaged or missing products

- **Damaged product:** in one reported case, the producer delivered another product to make up for the damaged one. No formal procedure was described.
- **Not enough products for everyone:** the group asked what happens when there is not enough of a product for all co-producers (for example, eggs). The group understood that, in that case, nobody receives that product, but the answer was not clear (to be confirmed).

### 3.6. Assembly

The stakeholder takes part in the AMAP's assembly. This indicates that AMAP UPTEC Baixa holds an assembly, which helps clarify the doubt recorded in the 29/09 session.

## 4. Relation to previously gathered information

- **Confirms:** several producers in the same AMAP, extra products ordered separately (eggs), the possibility of giving the basket to another person when the co-producer cannot receive it, and the existence of an assembly at AMAP Baixa.
- **Adds:** each producer has its own payment and communication rules; payment may depend on what was actually delivered; concrete payment methods exist (MB Way, bank transfer); and orders are placed well in advance through a form.

## 5. Implications to consider for the software

The following points come from the session but have not yet been validated as requirements:

- **Order form per period** with products from several producers, options per product and the possibility of selecting more than one.
- **Co-producers can view the orders they have placed**, which today is done through the copy sent by email.
- **Payment rules configurable per producer**: advance payment or payment based on what was actually delivered.
- **Recording payment methods** (MB Way, bank transfer).
- **Delivery dates set by the producer.**
- **Recording failures and compensations** (damaged or missing products).

## 6. Information mentioned but not yet sufficiently certain

- **Holidays:** it is unclear whether there is a rule about holidays (for example, no pauses during holidays, so the basket can be given to other people).
- **Insufficient products:** it is unclear whether, when a product is not enough for everyone, nobody receives it, it is shared among some, or it is compensated in another way.
- **Payment to the producer:** it is still unclear whether the co-producer pays each producer directly and who issues the invoice in each case.

## 7. Points to clarify in future sessions

- Who creates and manages the order form, and how are orders collected and sent to each producer?
- How is the amount to pay calculated when products are missing?
- What happens when a product is not enough for all co-producers?
- What is the rule about holidays and absences?
- Which decisions are made in the assembly, and how often does it meet?
- Which other producers take part in the AMAP, and which products do they supply?

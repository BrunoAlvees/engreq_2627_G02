**Updated on:** 07/10/2026

## 1. Answers to "to be confirmed" points in the summary

| Open point in the summary | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Holiday rule** (§6) | With the new **six-month commitment**, it is no longer possible to suspend the basket during holidays ("we banned holidays"). Absent co-producers arrange an alternative (e.g. a friend collects the basket); if there is none, the basket is **distributed among the other co-producers**. |
| **Not enough product for everyone** (§6) | For eggs, when there are not enough for everyone, **there are no partial deliveries within an AMAP** (to avoid inequality). One week one AMAP gets no eggs, the next week another one does, **in rotation**. The value of eggs not delivered is **deducted** from that month's payment. |
| **How the amount is calculated when products are missing** (§7) | At the end of each month, or early the next month, the producer sends a **private message** with the amount based on what was **actually delivered**. The stakeholder said that "full" risk sharing would keep the price fixed, but this does not happen yet. |
| **Who sets the dates** (§3.3) | Confirmed: **the producers**, at the start of each cycle. The semester's dates are set in advance (until December) and only change exceptionally (e.g. public holidays). |

## 2. Additional information

### 2.1. Order form and deadlines
- There is an **order deadline** tied to the semester's first delivery. Example: form released at the end of June, orders until the 4th, first delivery on the 7th.
- The form starts with the AMAP's **general operating rules**.
- The stakeholder **keeps the copy of the form** received by email and even copies the price tables into a note, so she knows **what she ordered and how much she owes**. Otherwise she has to ask the producer.

### 2.2. Products and rules per producer
- **Bread:** there are types with **different price levels** and **sizes** (small/large), including **gluten-free**. Changes are made by private message and **must be sent by Sunday**, because the dough starts fermenting on Monday for Tuesday's delivery.
- **Mushrooms** (new producer): two types, usually fresh. On cold days the producer **confirms on Monday** whether there will be any. **Dried mushrooms** are possible as an alternative, **but they are not in the form**.
- **Quinta da Serrinha** (olive oil): occasional deliveries (quarterly or per semester, to be confirmed), **handed over in person**; the producer has waited for and phoned co-producers who did not show up.
- **Baskets:** there are several types (fruit only, farm basket and another one, to be confirmed), some with more vegetables and others with more fruit. As understood by the group, a combination of two baskets costs around **€50 per month** (the farm basket alone costs €32, according to the 29/09 session). **To be confirmed.**
- Separate products (e.g. **beans**) are ordered by **private message** and paid separately.

### 2.3. Collection
- **Previously**, products were left in bulk with a **list on the wall** and each co-producer put together their own basket. As many did not show up, producers were left waiting.
- **Now** producers deliver **pre-packed bags labelled with the co-producer's name** (e.g. "Name 1 of 2", "2 of 2"). According to the stakeholder, this made the AMAP "a little less human" (discussed at the General Assembly on 05/10), but it made the process viable for producers.
- **There is no collection confirmation.** The space is not supervised. Baskets have been left until the next day, expensive products left in the heat, and someone else's bread taken. When someone else collects on a co-producer's behalf, there is also no way to confirm it was the right person. The stakeholder considers confirmation **useful for everyone**, especially if the AMAP grows.
- Not all producers are present at deliveries. Some run **routes across several delivery points** and used to wait up to two hours to hand over about 30 baskets.

### 2.4. Information the co-producer would like to have
- Knowing **what she will collect and how heavy it is**, because she walks (e.g. olive oil and nuts can add up to several kilos).
- **Delivery reminders**: some people forget.
- **Occasional surplus offers** (e.g. heirloom tomatoes) that today only arrive by private message, and **quantity management** by producers.
- **Easy access to the AMAP's rules**: there are documents and an AMAP email address, but they are hard to find.

### 2.5. Basket exclusions
- Handled **informally**: the co-producer tells the producer what she doesn't like and the producer swaps it. The producer also knows each person's preferences.

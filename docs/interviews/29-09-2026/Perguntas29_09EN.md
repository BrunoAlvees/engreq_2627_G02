## Level 1: open questions
- [ ] 1. Could you describe how your CSA works day to day?
- [ ] 2. Could you describe how your CSA works, from the moment a producer decides what to offer until the basket reaches the consumer?
- [ ] 3. What are the biggest difficulties you currently face in running the CSA?
- [ ] 4. What documents, lists or tools (Excel, email…) do you use today to organise everything? Could we see an example?
- [ ] 5. Who are the people involved and what is each one's role?
- [ ] 6. (already partly answered in class) Could you describe how the CSA works from the moment a consumer finds the programme until they receive their first basket?

## Level 2: more specific questions
**Payments and invoicing**
- [ ] 5. Could you explain how payments work: who pays whom, when and how?
- [ ] 6. Who issues invoices or receipts, and how does it work when there are several producers?

**When something goes wrong**
- [ ] 7. Could you tell us about a situation where something did not go as planned (lack of production, someone did not collect the basket…) and how it was solved?
- [ ] 8. How are periods of high and low production balanced over the year? How do you know it was fair to everyone?

**Co-producers**
- [ ] 9. What happens when a co-producer does not like a product in the basket, or cannot collect it?
- [ ] 10. How does a co-producer join and leave the CSA? Is there a limit on places?

**Communication and organisation**
- [ ] 11. How does information reach people (new offers, changes, delivery reminders)?
- [ ] 12. How much time does running the CSA take and which tasks are the most demanding?

**BioGoods (notes taken during the session)**

- Documents used: Excel
- Two types of AMAP: single-producer / multi-producer
- Product: farm basket ("cabaz da quinta")
- Subscription period of 6 months
- Monthly payments of €32
- Fortnightly basket of vegetables and fruit, about 4 kg
- It must be possible to set exceptions person by person
- 4 AMAPs work in alternating weeks
- Delivery option: fortnightly, or weekly by joining two AMAPs
- The day may differ (usually Tuesday) and can vary from AMAP to AMAP
- Each person joins the AMAP for a defined period, which can vary from AMAP to AMAP
- Delivery balancing: larger baskets can be delivered in summer
- Payment is made upfront for the whole subscription period or monthly
- Products outside the basket are possible, with a discount for subscribers
- People involved: deliveries, BioGoods management
- Assemblies, where decisions are made
- Each company issues its own invoice
- Exceptions when people are on holiday or do not collect the product: give it to a friend, donate it to an organisation, share the basket among the other co-producers; the basket must be collected and must be paid for
- Remind people about deliveries

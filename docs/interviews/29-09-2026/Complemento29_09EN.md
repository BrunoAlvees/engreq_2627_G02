## 1. Answers to "to be confirmed" points in the summary

| Open point in the summary | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Delivery balancing** (§5) | The stakeholder said this **probably has no impact on the software**. Co-producers are guaranteed a minimum quantity and variety over the 6 months. |

## 2. Additional information

### 2.1. Fixed monthly price
- The AMAP charges **per month, not per basket**. In months with five Tuesdays there is an extra delivery, but the monthly amount **does not change**.
- ⚠️ In the 06/10 session, the co-producer said the monthly amount is based on what was **actually delivered** (e.g. missing eggs are deducted). **To be confirmed** whether the basket has a fixed price and extras a variable one.

### 2.2. Communicating the basket contents
- Today the stakeholder sends **a WhatsApp message every week** with the basket contents. She would like co-producers to receive this **automatically**, by message or on the platform.
- The software should show **per person** what each co-producer will receive, taking exclusions into account.

### 2.3. Extra products
- The list of extra products (beans, eggs, chicken, lamb…) **should not be closed**: it is one thing today and may be another tomorrow.

### 2.4. Early commitment renewal
- Having co-producers with commitments of different lengths **affects** planning, because crops are planted in advance.
- **Commitment renewal should happen in advance** (about 3 months): crops take 60 to 100 days and, to deliver in January, the soil must be prepared now.

### 2.5. Absences: choosing what happens to the basket in the software
- The basket **must be collected and must be paid for**.
- The software should let the **co-producer state when they will be away and what they want to do with the basket**, choosing from **options defined** by the AMAP/producer (friend or relative, donation to an organisation, sharing among other members, a raffle, among others).

### 2.6. Collection confirmation
- Sometimes the producer only finds out **the next day** that someone did not collect their basket.
- Asked who should confirm collection, she answered **the co-producer**, because not all locations have someone present.

### 2.7. Invoicing: current and possible model
- **Today:** each farm issues its own invoice.
- **Possible in the future:** each producer invoices the AMAP, the AMAP pays the producers and handles all payments with co-producers.
- The stakeholder stressed that this **is not what exists, it is what could exist**, and that it is up to the group to decide how far to take the solution.

### 2.8. Delivery points
- Each BioGoods AMAP has one delivery point. A larger CSA (about 200 people) had **about 6 delivery points**, opened when there were at least 10 to 20 co-producers in an area.
- BioGoods chose to create **independent AMAPs** instead of several points of the same AMAP, so each one defines how it works.

## 2. Order form (D-A)

### 2.1. General structure

- The title gives the **AMAP and the period**: a **six-month** cycle (July to December 2026).
- It opens with the announcement of the subscription period, the **list of producers** and the **operating rules**.
- **4 producers**, each with its own section:

| Producer | Offer |
|---|---|
| Biogoods (Cinfães, certified organic) | Vegetables, pulses, eggs, fruit, herbs, chicken, lamb, jams, bee products |
| Trigo do Umbigo (bakers' collective, Ermesinde) | Artisan sourdough bread |
| Quinta da Serrinha (Alfândega da Fé, organic) | Own olive oil and olives; nuts, cheese and wine **from other local producers** (resale) |
| Cogumelos de Argoncilhe (certified organic) | Mushrooms |

- **Operation:** **fortnightly deliveries on Tuesdays, 17:30–19:30**, at the Pavilhão-Jardim of UPTEC Baixa (Porto).
- **Calendar fixed at the start** (13 dates): 7/7, 21/7, 4/8, 18/8, 1/9, 15/9, 29/9, 13/10, 27/10, 10/11, 24/11, 8/12, 22/12. **September has 3 deliveries.**
- **Order deadline:** 4 July (the first delivery is on 7/7). After the deadline, requests are sent by **email to the AMAP**, which **assesses whether** it can accept them.
- Co-producers bring their own bags (or return the producers' bags) and are **invited to help with the distribution**.
- Each producer has a **free-text "Notes" box**. Everything the form does not support goes there: different quantities, meat options, loose products, bread varieties.

### 2.2. Biogoods

| Product | Frequency | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Vegetable box** (~2.5–3 kg, 6 to 10 items) | Fortnightly | **€32/month** |
| **Farm box** (~3 kg: pumpkin, potato, sweet potato, onion, fruit) | Fortnightly | **€26/month** |
| **Fruit box** (~2.5 kg) | Fortnightly | **€15/month** |
| Eggs (½ dozen) | Fortnightly | €3/half dozen |
| Blueberries | Fortnightly, **July and August only** | €12/kg |
| Rosemary, bay leaf | Monthly (first fortnight) | €1/bunch |
| Chicken (half or whole) | Monthly, on its own dates | €12/kg |
| Honey, pollen, fig jam | Quarterly (21/7 and 13/10) | €8/500 g; €7/150 g; €4.40/unit |
| Red beans | **Single delivery** (13/10) | €2.40/500 g |

Rules stated by Biogoods:

- **Fixed monthly fee for boxes:** boxes now have a monthly fee that reflects the total number of deliveries in the period. Three boxes are no longer charged in months with 3 deliveries.
- **Box contents vary with the season** (e.g. in winter the vegetable box may include pumpkin or onion). The boxes do not overlap with each other.
- **Automatic adjustment:** if the boxes already include blueberries, the loose blueberry order is reduced to avoid duplication.
- **Limited availability** (vegetable box, eggs, meat): once the limit is reached, new members go on a **waiting list**.
- **Meat:** the actual weight is only known on delivery day, so it is **not paid in advance**. The co-producer chooses the dates they want to receive it and uses the notes to say whether they want giblets or blood and whether the piece should be whole or cut.
- Fruit, pumpkin, potato and onion can be ordered **loose** instead of as a box.
- If a co-producer cannot receive an order on a given date, they must contact the producer.
- **Payment at the start of each month** (except meat). Biogoods sends a **payment note** with the amount due. VAT included.

### 2.3. Trigo do Umbigo (bread)

- Fortnightly deliveries. The producer **will not attend two dates** of the calendar (21/7 and 4/8).
- **3 price tiers × 2 sizes × several varieties**, chosen in a grid:

| Tier | 500 g | 1000 g | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bread A | €2.80 | €5 | Semi-wholemeal wheat, rye, corn bread |
| Bread B | €3.30 | €5.50 | Seeds, seaweed, walnuts and raisins, olives |
| Bread C | €3.50 | €6 | **Gluten-free**, beetroot and walnuts, focaccia, acorn |

- Some tier C breads depend on the seasonal availability of ingredients and **need confirmation**.
- In the notes, co-producers can ask for other quantities or to **vary the bread during the cycle**.
- The change deadline mentioned in the 06/10 session (until Sunday for the Tuesday delivery) **does not appear on the form**.

### 2.4. Cogumelos de Argoncilhe

- Shiitake and Pleurotus, €6/500 g, fortnightly delivery.
- **Availability is confirmed the weekend before** each delivery. That is why **payment is made at each delivery**.
- Dried mushrooms, mentioned in the 06/10 session as an alternative, **do not appear on the form**.

### 2.5. Quinta da Serrinha

- **Only 2 deliveries in the period** (September and December), on a date to be set, with an **email notice** a few days before.
- Before the December delivery, the producer asks by email whether the order **stays the same as in September or changes**.
- Catalogue with olive oil (3 sizes), vinegar, olives, almonds, almond paste (3 sizes), sheep's cheese (regular or vacuum-packed) and wine, plus an **"Other"** free-text field.
- Includes **storage and shelf-life** information for the products.

### 2.6. Page 2: personal data, payments and consents

- **Data requested:** email (from the Google account), **name** and **mobile number** (mandatory). The tax number is not requested.
- **Notes for all producers**, e.g. **absence periods**. Absences are reported in free text.
- **Payment details:** "except in specific cases, payments should be made at the start of each month". **Each producer has its own methods:** MBWay and bank transfer; one of them accepts **cash** instead of bank transfer.
- **Contacts for each producer** (website, email, mobile number). "All matters regarding products/orders/payments should be sent **directly to each producer**."
- **Checkboxes:**
  1. acceptance of the **Terms and Conditions** (D-B);
  2. **optional consent** to use the data for **studies/surveys** on AMAPs, only after the credibility of the study has been checked and confidentiality is guaranteed;
  3. awareness of the **Guide for new co-producers** (D-C).
- At the end, **a copy of the answers is sent by email**.
- The form cannot be saved halfway (the "Save" feature is disabled).

## 3. Terms and Conditions (D-B)

### 3.1. Data protection

- The **data controllers are the producers** of the AMAP, each responsible for delivering the food they produce. **It is not the AMAP.**
- Data processed: **name, email, phone number and tax number (NIF)**.
- Purposes: promoting the farming projects, planning and selling, and **accounting, tax and administrative management**.
- Legal basis: **consent**.
- Data subject rights: **objection, information, access to a copy (in electronic format), rectification and erasure**. Requests are sent by email to the AMAP.
- Data is kept for periods that depend on the purpose and on legal deadlines. Processors may be used (invoicing, administrative services) and data may be disclosed to authorities (Tax Authority, courts…).

### 3.2. Registration and use

- The document already refers to a "**digital ordering platform**" (the form).
- Registration requires **explicit acceptance** of the terms and the privacy policy.
- **Registration is not a right:** the AMAP may refuse a registration.
- Users declare they are **at least 18 years old** and use their own means of payment.
- **Registration is only confirmed after the order is submitted and paid** under each producer's conditions.
- The terms may be changed by the producers, by consensus.

### 3.3. Distribution

- Producers arrive at 17:00 and co-producers are invited to help prepare the distribution.
- **After 19:30, uncollected boxes are left outside the pavilion, labelled with the name.** This explains the cases reported in the 06/10 session (produce left in the heat, bread taken by the wrong person).

## 4. Guide for new co-producers (D-C)

### 4.1. Principles

- An AMAP is a **direct partnership based on a human relationship**, in which risks, responsibilities and rewards are shared in a **long-term relationship**.
- Portuguese AMAPs belong to **REGENERAR** (Portuguese Network for Solidarity Agroecology), a member of the international **Urgenci** network, and follow a **Charter of Principles** (https://amap.movingcause.org/carta-de-principios/):
  1. **Agroecology**;
  2. **Human-scale relationship**: **no intermediaries** between producer and consumer;
  3. **Food as a common good**: shared responsibility in production, distribution and consumption, avoiding waste.

### 4.2. How it works

- Up to **one week before** each cycle, co-producers receive the form by email.
- **Each producer sets** the products, price and frequency.
- The guide advises **never deleting the copy** of the form, so as not to forget what was ordered and how much is owed to each producer. This difficulty was confirmed in the 06/10 session.
- A new co-producer, or one joining mid-cycle, can choose a **trial delivery**.

### 4.3. Payments

- **Each producer sets the payment method**, and payment is made **directly to the producer**.
- The co-producer must **identify the payment** (proof sent by email or name in the transfer description).
- **Annual fee of €10** per member, producers included, for a **common fund** of the AMAP. **Half goes to REGENERAR**; the rest covers general expenses (gatherings, leaflets…).

### 4.4. Collection

- The schedule must be respected. If unable to attend, the co-producer **warns the producers** or **sends someone they trust**.

### 4.5. Organisation: Working Groups

AMAP Porto is run by **volunteer producers and co-producers**, on a periodic and rotating basis. A **Coordinating Team** brings together the information from all groups.

| Group | Tasks | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinating team | Common fund (fees, records, annual financial report); representation in REGENERAR; relationship with UPTEC/PINC | Annual / quarterly |
| Welcome | Receive and integrate new members; introduction session | Quarterly / ongoing |
| Communication | AMAP email, email list, WhatsApp group, Google Drive, promotion | Weekly / ongoing |
| Distribution | Support distribution day, from receiving the food to cleaning up | Weekly, rotating |
| Order and payment management | Prepare and share the form; **print distribution sheets** | Quarterly / monthly |
| Assembly / meetings | Call meetings, write and share minutes | Quarterly / annual |
| "Party committee" | Gatherings, farm visits, fairs, workshops | Quarterly / annual |

### 4.6. Communication and data

- Main channels: **AMAP email** and **WhatsApp group**.
- Product matters are handled **directly between producer and co-producer**.
- Producers only use contact details within the AMAP, unless expressly authorised otherwise.

## 5. Público article (D-D)

- AMAP stands for **Associação para a Manutenção da Agricultura de Proximidade** (Association for the Maintenance of Local Agriculture). AMAP Porto has existed since **October 2016**. There are other AMAPs in the country (Gaia, Guimarães, Famalicão, Alvalade do Sado, Montemor-o-Novo…).
- **Horizontal organisation**: there is no hierarchy between producers and consumers, and risks and rewards are shared.
- In 2023: **34 co-producers**, a stable number of around 30; an internal survey found that **93 people** are fed by the AMAP.
- In 2023 the subscription was **quarterly**, paid at the start of the quarter or in instalments. **In 2026 it became six-monthly.**
- The list of producers changed a lot between 2023 and 2026: **producers come and go** between cycles.
- Motivations mentioned: for producers, **selling their produce, reducing waste** and ideology; for co-producers, **prices similar to or lower than supermarkets**, quality and **supporting local producers**.
- According to Biogoods, "people understand" when a product is missing or comes in larger or smaller amounts than expected.

## 6. Conclusions

| Topic | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| **Fixed or variable monthly amount** (difference between the 29/09 and 06/10 sessions) | Both accounts are right, depending on the product. **Boxes have a fixed monthly fee**; **extras are variable** (meat by weight, mushrooms per delivery). The monthly payment note adds everything up, so the total varies |
| **Box price** | The €32 mentioned on 29/09 is the **vegetable box**. There are 3 boxes: vegetable (€32), farm (€26) and fruit (€15) |
| **Stakeholders** | Besides producers and co-producers: the **Coordinating Team and Working Groups** (volunteer management), **REGENERAR**, **UPTEC/PINC** (venue) and **researchers** (consent for studies) |
| **Calendar** | There is a common AMAP calendar, but each producer may **skip dates** or have **its own dates** per product |
| **Payments** | Always **direct to each producer**, with different methods and timings and manual identification. The **annual fee** is a separate account |
| **Data protection** | The **data controllers are the producers**. Consents and data subject rights must be supported |
| **"No intermediaries" principle** | May limit the scope: a platform that charges commissions, or an AMAP that invoices on behalf of producers, may conflict with the Charter of Principles. **To be discussed by the group** |
| **Limitations of the current system** | A lot of information goes into **free text**; important rules **do not appear on the form**; co-producers rely on the **email copy** to know what they ordered and how much they owe |

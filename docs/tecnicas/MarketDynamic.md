# Technique 3: Market Dynamics and Competitive Analysis

**Timing:** Early elicitation (to discover baseline features) and validation (to compare proposed solutions against the market)

**Owner:** João Mata

**Status:** planned

## Why this technique
- Before building custom software, it is a core Requirements Engineering principle to investigate if the problem has already been solved and to avoid "reinventing the wheel".
- Stakeholders' expectations are often shaped by existing platforms they have used or seen. Understanding the current market establishes a baseline for user expectations.
- Analyzing existing AMAP/CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) platforms helps uncover "must-have" requirements that stakeholders might forget to mention because they assume they are standard.
- It helps answer a critical business question for BioGoods: *Why build a custom solution instead of buying/using an off-the-shelf system?*

## Objectives
**1.** **Identify existing software solutions** serving AMAPs, CSAs, and local food distribution networks (e.g., Open Food Network, Farmigo, CSAware, or local European alternatives).

**2.** **Create a feature comparison matrix** to see what capabilities are standard across the market (e.g., subscription management, producer portals, automated route planning).

**3.** **Identify the limitations of current market offerings** to understand exactly why BioGoods needs a custom solution (the "market gap").

**4.** **Discover innovative or "delighter" features** that competitors use, which can be proposed to BioGoods during future interviews.

**5.** **Formulate targeted questions** for stakeholders regarding specific platforms (e.g., "Have you tried platform X? What didn't work for you?").

## Sources to evaluate

- Direct competitor websites and product feature pages (at least **3**-**4** platforms)
- Open-source agricultural/food hub software repositories
- User reviews, forums, or communities where AMAP coordinators discuss software tools
- Demo accounts or sandbox environments of competitor software (if available)
- Case studies on digital transformation in short food supply chains

## Method
**1.** Conduct targeted web searches using keywords related to AMAP, CSA, and food hub management software.

**2.** Select **3** to **5** of the most relevant existing platforms to analyze.

**3.** Define evaluation criteria based on the domain (e.g., User Management, Basket Customization, Payment Gateways, Invoicing, Route Optimization).

**4.** Build a comparison matrix mapping which platforms have which features.

**5.** Analyze the strengths and weaknesses of these platforms from the perspective of an AMAP like BioGoods.

**6.** Extract a list of potential functional and non-functional requirements based on industry standards.

**7.** Prepare questions for the next stakeholder meeting to validate these findings.

## Expected results (evidence)
- List of analyzed market solutions / competitors.
- Competitor feature comparison matrix.
- List of baseline requirements extracted from market standards.
- Identified market gaps (justification for the custom software).
- Questions for the next interview regarding competitor software and standard features.
- Hours logged per team member.

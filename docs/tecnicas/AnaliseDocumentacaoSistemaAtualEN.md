# Technique 2: Document analysis and current system analysis

**Timing:** before elicitation (to prepare interviews) and after (to check requirements coverage)
**Owner:** Bruno Alves
**Status:** in progress

## Why this technique
- In the 23/09 session, the lecturer pointed out that no questions had yet been asked about the **documents** involved in the process, and that "documents tell things that people don't say in interviews" (e.g. an invoice contains a header, customer identification, products line by line, total and payment method).
- In the 29/09 interview, BioGoods mentioned that the AMAP is organised using **Excel**. That is the **current system** the solution will replace.
- It lets us gather information **without using stakeholders' time** on what is already written down.

## Objectives
1. **Collect real documents** used in the operation of AMAPs/CSAs.
2. **Identify the information the system must store**: entities and fields (co-producer, producer, AMAP, subscription, basket, delivery, payment, invoice).
3. **Understand how Excel is used today**: what works well, what fails, and which tasks are manual or error-prone.
4. **Identify legal and regulatory constraints** (invoices issued by each producer, personal data protection / GDPR).
5. **Prepare concrete questions** for the next interview based on the gaps found.
6. **(Later)** Check that the specified requirements **cover everything the current system does**.

## Documents to collect
- [ ] Excel file(s) used by BioGoods (anonymised version)
- [ ] Example of an invoice/receipt issued by a producer
- [x] AMAP membership form or contract: Jul–Dec 2026 order form and AMAP UPTEC Baixa Terms and Conditions
- [ ] AMAP charter of principles: summarised in the Guide for new co-producers; the full document still needs to be analysed
- [x] Internal rules of an AMAP: AMAP Porto Guide for new co-producers
- [x] Delivery lists / distribution calendar: calendar in the Jul–Dec 2026 order form
- [ ] Relevant legislation (invoicing, GDPR)

## Method
1. Request the documents from BioGoods and search for public documents (AMAP/CSA websites, AMAP network).
2. For each document, record: source, date, who produces it, who uses it and for what.
3. Extract the information fields into a common table.
4. Analyse the Excel file: which sheets exist, what data it stores, what calculations it does, what problems it has.
5. List doubts and gaps, which become questions for the next interview.

## Expected results (evidence)
- List of analysed documents, with their source.
- Table of extracted entities and fields.
- List of strengths and problems of the current system.
- List of candidate requirements and constraints.
- Questions for the next interview.
- Hours logged per team member.

# 3.3 Cycles and subscriptions

**Owner:** Bruno Alves · **Updated on:** 12/11/2026 · **Status:** draft for the report
**Sources:** [RF03_CiclosSubscricoesEN.md](../requisitos/RF03_CiclosSubscricoesEN.md) (requirements, sources and status) · acceptance criteria in [12.4](12.4_AcceptanceCriteriaEN.md)

## 3.3.1 Description
This feature organises the work in subscription cycles (currently six-monthly at AMAP Porto; quarterly or yearly in others) and lets the co-producer subscribe, in a single step, to the products of several producers, with their options and exclusions. It replaces the Google Form and the email copy that co-producers keep to remember what they ordered, and gives each producer the totals they need to plan production.

**Priority: High** (5 of its 7 requirements are Must).

## 3.3.2 Stimulus/Response Sequences

*[Figure: `docs/requisitos/diagramas/casos_uso/CasosUsoRF03EN.png`]*
*Figure 3 – Use cases of feature 3.3 (Cycles and subscriptions)*

*Table – Stimulus/response sequences of feature 3.3*

| User action (stimulus) | System response |
|---|---|
| AMAP management opens a cycle with its length and subscription deadline | Notifies the active members and accepts subscriptions until the deadline |
| The co-producer chooses baskets, extras, variants and dates from several producers | Shows a summary with the amounts per producer and, after confirmation, sends the subscription by email |
| The co-producer records an allergy or a product they do not want | Shows the exclusion to the producers who deliver to them, so they can record the substitution |
| The co-producer changes their order during the cycle | Accepts the change if it is within the producer's deadline; otherwise explains why it cannot be made |
| A subscription request arrives after the deadline | Keeps it pending until management approves or refuses it |
| The end of the cycle is approaching | Asks the co-producer whether they will renew, with the previous order pre-filled |
| The producer opens the production plan | Shows the totals ordered per product, variant, date and AMAP |

### Use case specifications
The two main use cases are detailed below.

*Table – Use case: Subscribe to a cycle (FR-18)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Subscribe to a cycle |
| **Description** | A co-producer subscribes, in a single step, to the products of several producers of their AMAP for the next cycle. |
| **Actors** | Co-producer (primary), Producer |
| **Preconditions** | 1. The co-producer is an active member of the AMAP.<br>2. The cycle is open and the subscription deadline has not passed. |
| **Main flow** | 1. The co-producer opens the cycle's subscription page.<br>2. The system shows the products of all the AMAP's producers, with prices, variants and available places.<br>3. The co-producer chooses, for each product, the quantity, the variant and, where applicable, the delivery dates.<br>4. The system shows a summary with the products, dates and estimated amount per producer.<br>5. The co-producer confirms the subscription.<br>6. The system records the subscription, sends a confirmation by email and updates each producer's totals. |
| **Alternative flows** | A1 (step 3): the co-producer records exclusions (allergies, intolerances, dislikes); the system asks for explicit consent for health data.<br>A2 (step 3): a product has no places left; the system offers its waiting list (FR-14). |
| **Exceptions** | 1. The deadline has passed: the request stays pending until management approves or refuses it.<br>2. The co-producer leaves before confirming: nothing is recorded. |
| **Postconditions** | 1. The subscription is active for the cycle and visible to the co-producer and to the producers concerned.<br>2. The payment requests are scheduled according to each producer's payment rules (FR-31, feature 3.5). |

*Table – Use case: Renew subscription (FR-22)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Renew subscription |
| **Description** | Before the cycle ends, the co-producer states whether they will continue, so producers can plan the next cycle. |
| **Actors** | Co-producer (primary), AMAP management |
| **Preconditions** | 1. The co-producer has an active subscription.<br>2. The renewal lead time set by the AMAP has been reached (e.g. 3 months before the new cycle). |
| **Main flow** | 1. The system notifies the co-producer that renewal is open.<br>2. The co-producer opens the renewal, with the previous order pre-filled.<br>3. The co-producer keeps or adjusts the products.<br>4. The co-producer confirms that they will renew.<br>5. The system records the answer and adds it to the producers' totals for the next cycle. |
| **Alternative flows** | A1 (step 4): the co-producer says they will not renew; the system records it and notifies management, who can offer the place to the waiting list.<br>A2 (step 5): the AMAP uses mandatory confirmation; the answer becomes the subscription for the next cycle. |
| **Exceptions** | 1. The co-producer does not answer by the deadline: with mandatory confirmation they lose their place to the waiting list; with intention only, the system sends a reminder. |
| **Postconditions** | 1. The co-producer's intention or confirmation for the next cycle is recorded. |

## 3.3.3 Functional Requirements

*Table – Functional requirements of feature 3.3 (M = Must, S = Should, C = Could)*

| ID | Requirement | Actor | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-17 | The system shall allow creating subscription cycles with a configurable length, an opening date and a subscription deadline. | AMAP management | M |
| FR-18 | The system shall allow the co-producer to subscribe, in a single step, to products from several producers of the same AMAP, with their options, and receive a confirmation. | Co-producer | M |
| FR-19 | The system shall allow the co-producer to record exclusions (allergies, intolerances, dislikes) and the producer to record the substitution made. | Co-producer, Producer | M |
| FR-20 | The system shall apply subscription deadlines and each producer's rules for changing orders during the cycle. | Co-producer, Producer, AMAP management | S |
| FR-21 | The system shall allow the co-producer to view, at any time, their current subscriptions and history. | Co-producer | M |
| FR-22 | The system shall ask co-producers in advance whether they will renew, starting from their previous order. | Co-producer, AMAP management | S |
| FR-23 | The system shall give the producer the information needed to plan production: totals ordered and aggregated preferences. | Producer | M |

*Acceptance criteria for each requirement are in Appendix 12.4.*

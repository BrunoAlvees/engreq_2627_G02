## 10.2.2 Context and results of each technique

### T1. Competitor analysis
- **Context:** the AMAP movement in Portugal is small and no Portuguese tool is made for AMAPs. Four solutions were compared, one per category.
- **Result:** organisation-focused tools (AMAPJ, Juntagrico) fit best, but none is in Portuguese. Commission on baskets conflicts with AMAP ethics. Market gaps: Portuguese interface, MB WAY or pay-at-pickup payment, one shared instance for all Portuguese AMAPs, minimal effort for farmers.
- **Status:** in progress (draft); Portuguese version pending.

### T2. Document and current system analysis
- **Context:** the lecturer pointed out that documents "say things that people do not say in interviews" (23/09). The producer uses Excel and WhatsApp (29/09).
- **Documents analysed:** order form (D-A), terms and conditions (D-B), guide for new co-producers (D-C), Público report (D-D), Charter of Principles (D-E), network FAQ (D-F).
- **Result:** information the system must store; differences between documents and interviews (C1 onwards); legal constraints (invoicing, GDPR, PSD2); candidate requirements DOC-01 to DOC-40; questions for the next sessions.
- **Limit:** still missing the **BioGoods Excel file**, a **monthly payment note** and an **invoice** example.

### T3. Market dynamics
- **Result so far:** plan, objectives, sources and method. **No findings yet** (feature matrix, market gap), so the status is still "planned".

### T4. Differentiation analysis
- **Result:** needs N1 to N19 from the interviews, classification, and differentiation proposals: D1 collection and absences, D2 payments with multiple producers, D3 personalised basket and information before delivery, D4 flexible orders and capacity, D5 software that keeps the personal relationship. They are **hypotheses**, not demonstrated advantages.

### T5. Interviews with stakeholders
| Date | Stakeholder | Perspective | Main outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23/09 | Lecturer as FAIR client | Client | FAIR's goal and scope, how AMAPs work, open points (E-1) |
| 29/09 | Sofia Araújo, BioGoods | Producer | Current system (Excel, WhatsApp), baskets, extras, calendar, absences, payments (E-2) |
| 06/10 | Luisa Marques, AMAP UPTEC Baixa | Co-producer | Order form, several producers with their own rules, payments, collection (E-3) |

The professor's advice was followed: open questions first, then specific ones; ask about documents; identify stakeholders.

### T6. Classes with the lecturer as client
Four short contributions that changed the scope: process for new co-producers to join (E-4), centralised collection of subscription money (E-5), **invoicing out of scope** (E-6) and the producer's application to an AMAP (E-7). E-4 and E-5 were not recorded.

### T7 to T9
Brainstorms set the guiding principles (14/10) and the scope decisions (centralised collection on 21/10, requirement reduction on 28/10). The consolidation gave **45 functional and 24 non-functional requirements**, each with its source.

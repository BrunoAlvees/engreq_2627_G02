## 12.3.2 E-1: Session with the FAIR client (23/09)

**Goal:** understand the business, the intended scope and how an AMAP/CSA works in general.
**Format:** project presentation followed by open questions from the group.

### Main statements

| Topic | What was said | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | FAIR does not want to create AMAPs; it wants software that helps existing AMAPs organise themselves, especially those that struggle. Aligned with the SDGs. | 1.3, FR-01 |
| Joining | The consumer looks for a nearby AMAP, or one they like, and asks for a place. If there is none, they go on a waiting list. | FR-02, FR-03 |
| Management | Can be one person or a committee, with or without a formal association. It follows payments, deliveries and collections, sets the calendar and deals with problems. | FR-06, FR-24 |
| Commitment | Subscription to a basket for a period (e.g. 6 months or 1 year), with seasonal products. The co-producer accepts that contents vary. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly deliveries. | FR-17, FR-24 |
| Offers | Producers state what they will produce, when and how much; co-producers subscribe. A 3-month cycle is common, but "the offer cycle and the commitment length are not necessarily the same". | FR-11, FR-17, PA-02 |
| Producers | They usually show certification of how they produce; small ones without a certificate show photos of the farm. | FR-04, PA-01 |
| Relationship | The co-producer shares responsibility for production. There are farm visits and sometimes help with farm work. | NFR-20 |
| Communication | Knowing the calendar does not replace reminders of upcoming deliveries. News about production is published. | FR-35, FR-37 |
| Payments | Usually at the start of the cycle, so the producer can invest. Invoicing depends on how the AMAP is set up. There is interest in reducing the number of payment operations. | FR-30, FR-31, FR-32 |
| Indicators | KPIs and reports useful for network organisations and researchers. | FR-43, FR-44 |
| FAIR model | Possibly a minimum fee plus a component linked to volume. | FR-34, NFR-21 |
| Maintenance | CSA software built by amateurs stops being maintained. | NFR-23 |

### To confirm after the session
- Rules for preferences and substitutions; how new offers are announced; balancing deliveries; FAIR's pricing model (who pays and how much).

### Lecturer's guidance on the process
- Start with open questions about the processes and only then go deeper.
- Ask for the documents used in the process (e.g. invoices, forms), because they show information that does not come up in interviews.
- Identify all stakeholders.

## 12.3.3 E-2: Session with the producer BioGoods (29/09)

**Goal:** understand the day-to-day work of a producer supplying several AMAPs in Porto.
**Format:** BioGoods presentation followed by questions prepared by the group, first open (level 1) and then specific (level 2).

### Questions and answers

| # | Question | Answer (summary) | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How does your AMAP work day to day? | One Excel file per AMAP, with co-producers, delivery dates and the monthly amount calculation. Communication by WhatsApp (individual messages or groups). | FR-31, FR-42, NFR-16 |
| 2 | From the producer deciding what to offer to the basket reaching the consumer? | Base product: the "farm basket", fortnightly, about 4 kg and 8 to 10 varieties (to confirm). Contents are not promised in advance. Extra products (e.g. eggs) sold separately, with a discount for basket holders. | FR-11, FR-12, FR-13, FR-25 |
| 3 | What are the biggest difficulties? | No single process is hard; the burden is the **number of tasks**: opening and closing orders, reading them, confirming them and reminding people of delivery dates. | FR-18, FR-35, NFR-02 |
| 4 | What documents and tools do you use? | Excel and WhatsApp. | FR-42, NFR-16 |
| 5 | Who is involved and what is each person's role? | The producer delivers and transports. AMAP Baixa has a yearly assembly; in the newer AMAPs the contact is the host institution's management (e.g. a school). | FR-06, FR-40 |
| 6 | How does a new co-producer join? | Through a subscription for a set period: usually 6 months, previously 3 months, 1 year in some AMAPs abroad. It may differ between AMAPs. | FR-03, FR-17 |
| 7 | How do payments work and who invoices? | Monthly, fixed amount (e.g. €32/month), even in months with fewer deliveries. Each farm issues its own invoice. | FR-31, NFR-18 |
| 8 | How are high and low production seasons balanced? | Bigger basket in summer, smaller in winter; about 13 deliveries per semester. Later: "probably has no impact on the software". | — |
| 9 | What happens when a co-producer dislikes a product or cannot collect the basket? | They can ask to exclude a product (allergy, intolerance or taste) and get another. If they cannot collect: give it to someone, donate it, share it among the others or raffle it. The basket must always be collected and paid for. | FR-19, FR-27 |
| 10 | How does a co-producer join and leave? Are places limited? | Pauses and drop-outs are rare and handled case by case. In the first year about 40% did not continue. | FR-07, FR-22 |
| 11 | How does information reach people? | A weekly WhatsApp message with the basket contents. | FR-25, FR-35 |
| 12 | How much time does the organisation take? | See answer 3. September is when harvests are planned. | FR-23 |

### Other relevant statements

| Topic | What was said | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| AMAP types | An AMAP can have one producer or several; the software must support both. | FR-01 |
| Calendar | AMAPs alternate weeks so the producer delivers every week. Those who want a weekly basket join two AMAPs. Deliveries are usually on Tuesday, but the day must be configurable. | FR-07, FR-24 |
| Length | "It should be built into our requirements": the subscription length must be configurable. | FR-17, NFR-24 |
| Renewal | Should happen about 3 months in advance, because crops take 60 to 100 days. | FR-22 |
| Extra products | The list must not be closed (beans, eggs, chicken, lamb…). | FR-11 |
| Basket contents | She would like co-producers to receive them automatically and the system to show, person by person, what each one gets after exclusions. | FR-19, FR-25 |
| Collection | Sometimes she only learns the next day that a basket was not collected. Confirmation should be done by the co-producer, because not every site has someone present. | FR-28 |
| Delivery points | A larger CSA (~200 people) had ~6 delivery points. BioGoods prefers independent AMAPs, each with its own way of working. | FR-01, NFR-09 |
| Future invoicing | Each producer could invoice the AMAP and the AMAP could handle payments with co-producers: "it is not what exists, it is what could exist". | C6, NFR-19 |
| Multi-producer baskets | Would be ideal, but uncommon in Portugal for lack of scale. | — (out of scope) |

## 12.3.4 E-3: Session with the AMAP UPTEC Baixa co-producer (06/10)

**Goal:** hear the point of view of a co-producer who also takes part in running the AMAP.
**Format:** questions from the group and analysis of the order form (Google Forms, July to December) shown by the stakeholder.

### Document analysed: order form
- Brings together several producers (BioGoods first), with products that have several options (e.g. two kinds of mushrooms, both selectable) and separate products (e.g. eggs).
- Starts with the AMAP's general rules and includes the contacts of the various AMAPs.
- Has a deadline tied to the first delivery (e.g. opened at the end of June, orders until the 4th, first delivery on the 7th).
- After submitting, the co-producer gets a copy by email. The stakeholder keeps it, and even copies the prices into a note, to know what she ordered and how much she owes.

Related requirements: FR-17, FR-18, FR-21, FR-39.

### Questions and answers

| # | Question | Answer (summary) | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do payments work? | It depends on each producer. BioGoods sends, at the end of the month by private message, the amount for what was **actually delivered**. Quinta da Serrinha (olive oil) prefers payment **before** delivery. Payment by MB Way or bank transfer. | FR-30, FR-31, NFR-17 |
| 2 | Who sets the delivery dates? | The producers, at the start of each cycle. The semester's dates are fixed and only change by exception (e.g. public holidays). | FR-24 |
| 3 | What happens during holidays? | With the six-month commitment, "we banned holidays": whoever is away arranges an alternative; if there is none, the basket is shared among the others. | FR-27 |
| 4 | And when a product is not enough for everyone? | For eggs, there are no partial deliveries within the same AMAP; AMAPs go without eggs in rotation and the amount is deducted. | FR-26, FR-31 |
| 5 | And when a product is spoiled? | In one case the producer compensated with another product. There is no formal procedure. | FR-26 |
| 6 | How does collection work? | Before: loose products and a list on the wall, with producers waiting for people who did not show up. Now: bags prepared with the co-producer's name, which made the AMAP "a little dehumanised". There is no collection confirmation: baskets have been left until the next day and someone else's bread has been taken. | FR-28, FR-29, N19 |
| 7 | How are exclusions handled? | Informally: the co-producer tells the producer what she dislikes and the producer swaps it. The producer knows everyone's preferences. | FR-19, AS-04 |
| 8 | What information would you like to have? | What she will collect and how much it weighs (she walks); delivery reminders; one-off surplus offers; easy access to the AMAP's rules. | FR-16, FR-25, FR-35, FR-39 |

### Rules per producer (statements)

| Producer | Rule | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Bread | Several types, prices and sizes (including gluten-free). Changes by private message **until Sunday**, because the dough starts proving on Monday for Tuesday's delivery. | FR-12, FR-20 |
| Mushrooms | Two kinds, fresh. On cold days the producer confirms on Monday whether there will be any. Dried mushrooms as an alternative, not on the form. | FR-12, FR-26 |
| Quinta da Serrinha | Olive oil, occasional delivery and **only by hand**; contacts by email and message and even phones people who do not show up. | FR-24, FR-37 |
| Separate products | E.g. beans, ordered by private message and paid separately. Surplus (e.g. ox-heart tomatoes) also only by private message. | FR-16 |

### To confirm after the session
- Fixed monthly amount (E-2) or variable according to what was delivered (E-3); perhaps fixed for the basket and variable for extras.
- Basket types and their prices; how often Quinta da Serrinha delivers.

# 12.4 Appendix – Acceptance criteria

**Owner:** Bruno Alves · **Updated on:** 12/11/2026 · **Status:** features 3.1 and 3.2 done
**Sources:** the requirement files in [requisitos/](../requisitos/README.md) (RF01, RF02), updated after the interview of 10/11 (E-8)

This appendix lists the acceptance criteria of each functional requirement in section 3. They detail what each requirement must do and how it can be verified.

## 12.4.1 Feature 3.1 – AMAP and member management

#### FR-01
- An AMAP has a name, delivery point, distribution day and time, contact and operating rules.
- To join the platform, an AMAP registers with its name, location, representative and tax number (NIF) and submits the requested documents; the FAIR administrator checks them and authorises the AMAP.
- There can be several independent AMAPs on the platform, and a producer can take part in several.
- An AMAP can have a single producer or several, with the same or different products.
- The AMAP has a maximum capacity of co-producers, limited by its premises, transport and space (e.g. 100).
- A rules panel lets management configure what varies between AMAPs: cycle length, subscription deadlines, number of trial deliveries, renewal mode (FR-22), basket destinations during absences (FR-27), mid-period withdrawal rules (FR-07) and documents (Terms, Guide).

#### FR-02
- The AMAP page shows the presentation, principles, producers and products, place and time, and whether there are places.
- The page has an "I want to join" option, which starts the joining request (FR-03).
- *(Could)* The portal lists all AMAPs on the platform, with search by location.

#### FR-03
- The system records who handles the request (main producer or management).
- If there are no places, the person goes on a waiting list ordered by arrival; management may change the order, recording the reason; the person is notified when a place opens.
- The number of trial deliveries before subscribing is configurable per AMAP (e.g. 1 or 2).
- Management can approve or reject the request.
- The system stores each person's status: prospective, on waiting list, on trial, active or left.

#### FR-04
- The co-producer registers with name, email and phone; the tax number (NIF) is only requested if they want an invoice with NIF.
- The producer's public profile shows a description, location, contacts and evidence of how they produce (certificates or photos and a description of the farm).
- The evidence is visible to co-producers; AMAP management assesses it in the producer's application, usually with a farm visit (FR-08).

#### FR-05
- Registration requires accepting the AMAP's Terms and Conditions and Guide, with a declaration of legal age; the accepted version and date are stored.
- Members can give and withdraw optional consents (e.g. use of data in studies), with proof of date and version.
- Data subjects can view, export, correct and request deletion of their data; the system tracks the response deadline (1 month).
- Each AMAP states the data controllers (the producers) and the contact point.

#### FR-06
- Roles: co-producer, producer, AMAP management and FAIR administrator, each with different permissions.
- Within each AMAP, AMAP management assigns and removes roles; FAIR does not decide on the AMAPs' internal organisation.
- The FAIR administrator only acts at platform level: authorises new AMAPs, assigns their first manager and provides support.
- One person can have several roles. If a producer starts the AMAP, they hold both the management and producer roles: they apply as a producer and accept the application as management.

#### FR-07
- A co-producer can be a member of several AMAPs (e.g. to get deliveries every week) and see all their deliveries in a single calendar.
- As a rule, a co-producer leaves at the end of the subscription period.
- Leaving mid-period is a withdrawal request that must be accepted; if accepted, the amount for the remaining deliveries is refunded. Who accepts it and what happens if it is refused are configurable per AMAP.
- When the withdrawal is accepted, the producers concerned are notified to stop the deliveries and the reason is recorded; alternatively, the next deliveries can be transferred to a substitute or donated to an organisation.

#### FR-08
- The producer applies to an AMAP, or is invited by management to apply.
- The application states name, location, main products and farming method (organic or not) and allows uploading documents (e.g. organic certificate or other certifications), which become part of the public profile (FR-04).
- *(Optional)* AMAP members usually visit the farm before deciding; if the visit takes place, it is recorded (date, who went, notes).
- Management or the assembly approves or rejects the application.
- An approved producer becomes active or, if the AMAP has no capacity, goes on the waiting list.
- Once active, the producer states the dates they can deliver (FR-24) and what they can deliver (FR-11).

#### FR-09
- The producer announces their departure with the notice period set by the AMAP's Terms; by default, they keep delivering until the end of the period already paid for and become inactive at the end of the subscription period.
- If the producer leaves mid-period, their planned deliveries are cancelled and accounts are settled or refunds made.
- From the leaving date, the producer no longer appears in new cycles, the catalogue or the calendar; future charges are cancelled and the affected co-producers are notified.
- If the producer leaves without notice, the system calculates, for each co-producer, the amount paid and not delivered; other producers may voluntarily compensate it, and the system records the compensation or the refund status (pending or refunded).
- The inactive producer's profile and history are kept for the accounts and can be reactivated if they return; if they are in other AMAPs, they stay active there.

#### FR-10
- Management asks to leave and sets the end date, preferably at the end of a cycle; members are notified in advance.
- Before the end date, management can export all the AMAP's data (members, subscriptions, deliveries, payments) as CSV or XLSX.
- On the end date, the AMAP becomes inactive: the public page is hidden and all charges stop.
- After a grace period, personal data is deleted or anonymised; only aggregate figures remain for the indicators.

## 12.4.2 Feature 3.2 – Catalogue and offers

#### FR-11
- The producer creates, edits and deactivates products; there is no closed list of products.
- Each product is one of three types: basket, extra or one-off.
- When preparing a new cycle, the producer can reuse the previous cycle's catalogue and only adjust prices and dates.

#### FR-12
- A producer can offer several basket types (e.g. farm basket, fruit basket), each with a name, description, approximate weight, number of varieties and price.
- A product can have variants (e.g. price level, size, variety, gluten-free), each with its own price.
- *(Could)* The producer can state alternatives to a product (e.g. dried mushrooms when there are no fresh ones).

#### FR-13
- Price types: fixed monthly fee, price per unit, and price per kg with the actual weight recorded at delivery.
- Frequency: fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, one-off delivery, only in certain months, or on dates chosen by the co-producer.
- *(Could)* The producer can set a discount on extra products for those who already subscribe to a basket.

#### FR-14
- Capacity is set at two levels: the AMAP's maximum capacity (FR-01) and the number of co-producers each producer can supply per product (e.g. two fruit producers with 25 and 30 baskets: 55 co-producers can subscribe to fruit).
- When capacity is reached, the system stops accepting subscriptions and offers the waiting list.

#### FR-15
- The details sheet shows the description, certification, storage and shelf life, origin (own production or another producer's) and price with VAT included.
- The producer's details are on their public profile (FR-04).

#### FR-16
- The producer can limit the offer to a maximum number of co-producers (first come, first served) and/or a maximum quantity per person.
- The producer states the available quantity; the system deducts it as orders arrive and closes the offer when it runs out; the producer can adjust the quantity at any time.

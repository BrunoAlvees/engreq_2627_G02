# 12.1 Glossary

**Owner:** Jakub Piwko · **Updated on:** 14/11/2026 · **Status:** draft for review
**Sources:** sections 1 to 11 of this SRS, [requisitos/](../requisitos/README.md), sessions E-1 to E-8 ([12.3](12.3_DetalheDadosElicitacaoEN.md))

This appendix defines the terms needed to read this SRS: AMAP domain terms, terms used for the requirements and the backlog, and acronyms. Terms are in alphabetical order. General software terms are only included when this document uses them with a specific meaning. Requirement identifiers (FR, NFR, AS, DE, L) and source codes (E-n, D-x, DOC-nn, DV-nn, PA-nn, …) are explained in section 1.2 and are not repeated here.

## 12.1.1 Terms

| Term | Definition | Ref. |
|---|---|---|
| **Absence** | A delivery date on which a co-producer cannot collect their basket. The co-producer chooses what happens to it from the AMAP's options (a friend collects it, donation, sharing among the others, raffle); the basket is always collected and paid for. | FR-27 |
| **AMAP** | *Associação para a Manutenção da Agricultura de Proximidade*. A local partnership in which co-producers commit in advance to buy the production of one or more producers for a period, with periodic deliveries at a fixed place and time. Used as a synonym of CSA. | 1.1, AS-05 |
| **AMAP management** | The person, committee or working group that runs an AMAP, usually volunteer co-producers. In an AMAP started by a producer, that producer. | 2.2, E-8 |
| **AMAP network** | The national network of AMAPs, which publishes the Charter of Principles and common information (D-E, D-F). A stakeholder, not a user class. | 10.1 |
| **Basket** | The main subscribed product: a set of seasonal products from one producer, delivered periodically (e.g. a farm basket of about 4 kg). The contents vary with the season and are not promised in advance. Baskets combining several producers are out of scope. | FR-11, FR-12, 1.3 |
| **Basket contents** | The list of products in a basket on a given delivery date, published by the producer before the delivery. | FR-25 |
| **Capacity** | The maximum number of co-producers, set at two levels: the AMAP (premises, transport) and each producer per product. When it is reached, new requests go to the waiting list. | FR-01, FR-14, E-8 |
| **Centralised collection** | Payment model in which the co-producer makes one payment per period, which a licensed payment provider splits among the producers. The system does not hold the money. | FR-32, NFR-19 |
| **Charter of Principles** | Document of the AMAP network that states the principles of AMAPs (D-E). FAIR-AMAP must respect it and must not act as a commercial intermediary. | NFR-20, FR-39 |
| **Co-producer** | A consumer who subscribes to an AMAP's products and shares responsibility for production with the producer. | 1.2, 2.2 |
| **Collection** | The moment the co-producer, or someone on their behalf, picks up their basket at the distribution point. It is confirmed in the app; baskets not confirmed by the end of the distribution slot are flagged. | FR-28 |
| **Commitment period** | The time for which a co-producer commits to an AMAP. It may differ from the length of the offer cycle. | FR-17, AS-13 |
| **Compensation** | What a producer gives for a missing item: a discount or a substitution in another delivery. | FR-26 |
| **Cycle (subscription cycle)** | The period for which producers offer products and co-producers subscribe, with an opening date and a subscription deadline (e.g. 3, 6 or 12 months). | FR-17 |
| **Delivery mode** | How a producer delivers: a labelled bag left at the distribution point, hand delivery, or a route across several points. | FR-24 |
| **Distribution point** | The place where baskets are delivered and collected, usually in a host institution. Also called pick-up point. | 2.3, FR-24 |
| **Distribution sheet / bag labels** | Printable A4 PDFs: the list of what each co-producer takes from each producer on a date, and the labels for each bag (name, "bag x of y", producer, date and, optionally, a QR code; never health data). Printing is optional. | FR-29, NFR-06 |
| **Distribution slot** | The weekday and time window in which an AMAP distributes baskets (e.g. Tuesdays 17:30–19:30). | FR-24 |
| **Exclusion** | A product a co-producer does not want to receive (allergy, intolerance or dislike). Allergies and intolerances are health data. | FR-19, NFR-06 |
| **Extra product** | A product sold in addition to the basket (e.g. eggs, bread), with its own price type and frequency. | FR-11, FR-13 |
| **FAIR (FAIR Software Solutions)** | The startup that is the client of this project and will run the platform. | 1.1 |
| **FAIR administrator** | FAIR staff member who manages the platform: authorises new AMAPs, gives support and configures FAIR's charges. | 2.2, FR-01, FR-34 |
| **Farm visit** | A visit by AMAP members to the farm of a producer who applied, before management decides. Usual but optional. | FR-08, E-7, E-8 |
| **Favoured user class** | A user class whose needs take priority when they conflict with those of other classes (co-producer, producer, AMAP management). | 2.2 |
| **Host institution** | An organisation that hosts a distribution point (e.g. UPTEC, a school). A stakeholder, not a user class. | 10.1, 2.3 |
| **Member status** | The stage of a person in an AMAP. Co-producer: prospective, on trial, on the waiting list, active, inactive. Producer: applicant, on the waiting list, active, inactive. | FR-03, FR-08 |
| **Missing item** | A subscribed product that is not delivered on a date (e.g. no mushrooms on cold days). It is compensated and not charged. | FR-26, FR-31 |
| **One-off offer** | A product offered outside the subscription (e.g. a surplus or a new product), with an available quantity; it closes when the quantity runs out. | FR-16 |
| **Payment period** | The interval at which a co-producer pays (e.g. a month). It is different from the cycle. | FR-31 |
| **Payment provider (licensed)** | A regulated third-party company (e.g. Stripe, or MB Way through an aggregator) that collects payments and splits them among producers. | DE-01, NFR-17 |
| **Persona** | A fictional person who represents a user class and makes its needs concrete (Ana, Rui, Marta). | 2.2 |
| **Pilot** | The first phase of FAIR-AMAP in Portugal, with the platform almost free of charge, to collect feedback from AMAPs. | 1.3, NFR-21 |
| **Price type** | How a product is charged: fixed monthly fee, price per unit, or price per kg with the actual weight recorded at delivery. | FR-13 |
| **Producer** | A farmer or processor who supplies one or more AMAPs. | 1.2, 2.2 |
| **Production plan** | The totals ordered per product, variant, delivery date and AMAP, which the producer uses to plan production and harvesting. | FR-23 |
| **Prospective co-producer** | A person who wants to join an AMAP and has no active membership yet. | 2.2, FR-02, FR-03 |
| **Release** | A group of backlog stories delivered together: MVP, R2 and R3 (section 11). | 11 |
| **Renewal** | Asking co-producers, before a cycle ends, whether they continue, starting from their previous order. Each AMAP chooses intention (indicative) or mandatory confirmation by a deadline. | FR-22, AS-15 |
| **Role** | A set of permissions a person has in one AMAP (e.g. management, producer, co-producer). One person may have several roles. | FR-06 |
| **Story point** | Relative unit used to estimate the effort of a user story, on the scale 1, 2, 3, 5, 8; 1 SP ≈ 4 hours (section 11). | 11 |
| **Substitution** | A product given instead of an excluded or missing one, recorded by the producer. | FR-19, FR-26 |
| **Terms and Conditions** | The AMAP's document that a co-producer accepts when joining (e.g. D-B); the version accepted by each member is stored. | FR-05, FR-39 |
| **Trial deliveries** | Deliveries a new co-producer receives before subscribing to a cycle. Not confirmed by the client. | FR-03, AS-11 |
| **User class** | A group of people who use FAIR-AMAP in a similar way; a subset of the stakeholders. | 2.2 |
| **User story** | A short description of a feature from a user's point of view: *As a <user class>, I want <goal>, so that <benefit>*. | 11 |
| **Variant** | A version of a product with its own price (e.g. size, variety, gluten-free). | FR-12 |
| **Waiting list** | The ordered list of co-producers or producers waiting for a place when capacity is reached; they are notified when a place opens. | FR-03, FR-14 |
| **Working group** | A group of AMAP members with assigned tasks (e.g. new members, calendar). | FR-40, DV-14 |

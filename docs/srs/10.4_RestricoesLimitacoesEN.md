# 10.4 Constraints and limitations

**Owner:** Liane Duarte · **Updated on:** 11/11/2026 · **Status:** updated with the session of 10/11 (E-8), the last one with the client before delivery
**Sources:** [interviews](../interviews/), source codes in [requirements/README.md](../requisitos/README.md), [doubts about current processes](../tecnicas/DuvidasProcessosAtuaisEN.md) (DV-nn), [open questions](../requisitos/PerguntasEmAbertoEN.md) (PA-nn)

This section describes the constraints and problems that came up **during elicitation** and that limit how far some requirements can be trusted. For each one it states the effect and what the group did to reduce it. **Product** constraints are in section 2.4, and the assumptions that follow from these limitations are in section 2.5.

## 10.4.1 Access to stakeholders

| # | Limitation | Effect on the requirements | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Few and short sessions.** The group took part in four formal sessions (23/09, 29/09, 06/10 and 10/11), held during classes (theory and PL) and shared with the other groups. The last one (E-8) lasted 30 minutes and only covered FAIR and an AMAP's management; no producer was interviewed. | Doubts remained open, mostly on the producer's side and on management's day-to-day work: most DVs, PA-02 and part of PA-04. E-8 answered DV-02, DV-25, PA-01 and PA-03, and part of DV-26. Some MoSCoW priorities are group proposals rather than the client's. | Questions were prepared before each session and ordered by priority (DVs marked High). Each requirement's source and status are recorded in the project repository, so it is clear what is validated and what is a group proposal. |
| L2 | **Few real stakeholders.** One producer (BioGoods) and one co-producer were interviewed, both from the same Porto AMAP network. An AMAP's management was only heard in E-8, played by the lecturer. It was not possible to talk to other producers (bread, mushrooms, Quinta da Serrinha) or AMAPs in other regions. A session with a second BioGoods stakeholder (José Costa, responsible for vegetable production), announced for the theory class of 03/11 and held a week later, was not attended by any group member, so its content is not available. | The described way of working may be specific to the AMAP UPTEC Baixa. AMAP management requirements (FR-01, FR-06, FR-40) rely on documents and a single session with management, and the producer's view relies on a single session (E-2). | Document analysis of other sources from the network (Charter of Principles, Público article, Quinta da Serrinha presentation) and competitor analysis of foreign solutions. Differences between AMAPs are handled by configuration (NFR-24), which E-8 confirmed ("each AMAP with its own rules"). The producer's questions that remained open were recorded for a future iteration (section 4 of the interview of 10/11). |
| L3 | **The lecturer acts as both client and lecturer.** Part of the information was given in class, outside the formal sessions (E-4, E-5, E-6, E-7), and it is not always clear whether it was said as the FAIR client. | It is not certain that these statements are client requirements. They affect joining (FR-03), producer entry (FR-08), invoicing (NFR-18) and centralised collection (NFR-19). | Each statement has its own source code. The doubt was recorded (DV-26), and so was the assumption (AS-11 in section 2.5). **Largely resolved in E-8:** producer entry, the waiting list and centralised collection were confirmed in a formal session. Trial deliveries (FR-03) remain unconfirmed. |
| L4 | **Indirect or unclear information.** The statement on centralised collection (E-5) was overheard in a conversation between the lecturer and another group, not in one of the group's own sessions. In the first session (23/09), some parts were unclear and were noted "with uncertainty". In E-8, two answers were unclear (the names of the certifications and what happens when a co-producer's withdrawal is refused). | An important scope decision (C6, payment model) rested on an indirect source. | The 23/09 session was revised (v2) and uncertain information was kept apart from confirmed information. **E-5 was confirmed directly in E-8.** The unclear answers from E-8 are flagged in the summary, and the affected rules were made configurable per AMAP (AS-14). |
| L5 | **No more sessions before delivery.** E-8 was the last chance to talk to the client before the SRS was delivered, and the questions left out could not be asked. | Some requirements could not be validated and remain a group proposal: renewal (FR-22), trial deliveries (FR-03), an AMAP leaving and data retention (FR-10), a producer's notice period (FR-09) and quality targets (NFR-02, NFR-03, NFR-10). | What remained unanswered became explicit group assumptions (AS-13 to AS-16 in section 2.5) and, where possible, rules configurable per AMAP, so they can be corrected without changing the software. The affected requirements stay "Proposed" and the questions were recorded for a future iteration (section 4 of the [interview of 10/11](../interviews/10-11-2026/Entrevista10_11EN.md)). |

## 10.4.2 Quality and consistency of the information

| # | Limitation | Effect on the requirements | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L6 | **Unclear or incomplete answers** on day-to-day rules: holidays, products that do not reach everyone, balancing deliveries, assemblies. | The delivery and exception requirements (FR-26, FR-27) describe what the system must allow, not fixed rules. | Rules were made configurable per AMAP and per producer (NFR-24), and the doubts were recorded as DVs. |
| L7 | **Differences between sources.** The AMAP documents and the interviews do not always match: basket weight, payment timing, invoicing, tax number (C1 to C9). Some documents are old (e.g. a 2023 article). | It is not known what current practice is in every case. | The differences were cross-checked in the document analysis (technique 2, reference [26] in section 1.4). Where the interview was more recent, it prevailed. The rest remain as doubts. |
| L8 | **Scope open on the client's side until E-8.** Payment intermediation (C6) and FAIR's pricing model (DV-25) were only decided in the last session (10/11). | Until then, the payment requirements (FR-30 to FR-34, NFR-17, NFR-19 to NFR-21) were "To validate" and were the hardest to estimate in the backlog. | The group chose an option (21/10), justified it in section 2.4 and flagged the affected requirements. **Resolved in E-8:** FAIR accepted centralised collection and defined the pricing model (fee per AMAP plus a percentage). Compatibility with the Charter of Principles was not discussed and remains a group decision. |

## 10.4.3 Constraints of the work itself

| # | Limitation | Effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L9 | **Too many requirements in the first version.** The first consolidation gathered 135 candidate requirements, with duplicates and different levels of detail. | They had to be reduced and renumbered twice (28/10 and 30/10), which means cross-references between documents must be checked. | Reduced to 44 FR and 24 NFR following the lecturer's advice (45 FR after FR-45 was added on 05/11), with mapping tables between old and new IDs in each file. |
| L10 | **Limited time and parallel work** with other courses, in a group of five people writing the same document. | Risk of inconsistencies between sections written by different people. | Each person writes their section in separate `.md` files (PT and EN) in a shared repository and pastes it into the shared Word document, using its styles. |
| L11 | **Public market sources only.** The competitor analysis (technique 1) and the review of example solutions (technique 4) used only public information (websites, demos, articles). The market dynamics technique (technique 3) has no results. It was not possible to try the solutions from the inside or to get market figures for AMAPs in Portugal. | The proposed differentiation is an estimate, and the market potential was not estimated. | Needs and differentiators were linked to validation questions for the stakeholders, and the answers from E-8 are recorded in technique 4. A feature that the sources do not mention is not treated as proven to be missing. |

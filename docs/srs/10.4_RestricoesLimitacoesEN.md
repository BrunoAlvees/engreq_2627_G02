# 10.4 Constraints and limitations

**Owner:** Liane Duarte · **Updated on:** 05/11/2026 · **Status:** draft, to be updated after the session in the week of 02/11
**Sources:** [interviews](../interviews/), source codes in [requirements/README.md](../requisitos/README.md), [doubts about current processes](../tecnicas/DuvidasProcessosAtuaisEN.md) (DV-nn), [open questions](../requisitos/PerguntasEmAbertoEN.md) (PA-nn)

This section describes the constraints and problems that came up **during elicitation** and that limit how far some requirements can be trusted. For each one it states the effect and what the group did to reduce it. **Product** constraints are in section 2.4, and the assumptions that follow from these limitations are in section 2.5.

## 10.4.1 Access to stakeholders

| # | Limitation | Effect on the requirements | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Few and short sessions.** There were three formal sessions (23/09, 29/09 and 06/10), held during practical classes and shared with the other groups. The next session is 30 minutes in total. | Many doubts remain open (DV-01 to DV-28 and PA-01 to PA-04). Some MoSCoW priorities are group proposals rather than the client's. | Questions were prepared before each session and ordered by priority (DVs marked High). Each requirement states its source and status, so it is clear what is not yet validated. |
| L2 | **Few real stakeholders.** One producer (BioGoods) and one co-producer were interviewed, both from the same Porto AMAP network. It was not possible to talk to an AMAP's management, other producers (bread, mushrooms, Quinta da Serrinha) or AMAPs in other regions. | The described way of working may be specific to the AMAP UPTEC Baixa. AMAP management requirements (FR-01, FR-06, FR-40) rely mostly on documents rather than interviews. | Document analysis of other sources from the network (Charter of Principles, Público article, Quinta da Serrinha presentation) and competitor analysis of foreign solutions. Differences between AMAPs are handled by configuration (NFR-24). |
| L3 | **The lecturer acts as both client and lecturer.** Part of the information was given in class, outside the formal sessions (E-4, E-5, E-6, E-7), and it is not always clear whether it was said as the FAIR client. | It is not certain that these statements are client requirements. They affect joining (FR-03), producer entry (FR-08), invoicing (NFR-18) and centralised collection (NFR-19). | Each statement has its own source code. The doubt was recorded (DV-26), and so was the assumption (AS-11 in section 2.5). |
| L4 | **Indirect or unclear information.** The statement on centralised collection (E-5) was overheard in a conversation between the lecturer and another group, not in one of the group's own sessions. In the first session (23/09), some parts were unclear and were noted "with uncertainty". | An important scope decision (C6, payment model) rests on an indirect source. | The 23/09 session was revised (v2) and uncertain information was kept apart from confirmed information. E-5 is flagged to be confirmed directly with the lecturer. |

## 10.4.2 Quality and consistency of the information

| # | Limitation | Effect on the requirements | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L5 | **Unclear or incomplete answers** on day-to-day rules: holidays, products that do not reach everyone, balancing deliveries, assemblies. | The delivery and exception requirements (FR-26, FR-27) describe what the system must allow, not fixed rules. | Rules were made configurable per AMAP and per producer (NFR-24), and the doubts were recorded as DVs. |
| L6 | **Differences between sources.** The AMAP documents and the interviews do not always match: basket weight, payment timing, invoicing, tax number (C1 to C9). Some documents are old (e.g. a 2023 article). | It is not known what current practice is in every case. | The differences were cross-checked in the document analysis (results, §4.2). Where the interview was more recent, it prevailed. The rest remain as doubts. |
| L7 | **Scope still open on the client's side.** Payment intermediation (C6) and FAIR's pricing model (DV-25) are not decided. | The payment requirements (FR-30 to FR-34, NFR-17, NFR-19 to NFR-21) are "To validate" and are the hardest to estimate in the backlog. | The group chose an option (21/10), justified it in section 2.4 and flagged the affected requirements. |

## 10.4.3 Constraints of the work itself

| # | Limitation | Effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| L8 | **Too many requirements in the first version.** The first consolidation gathered 135 candidate requirements, with duplicates and different levels of detail. | They had to be reduced and renumbered twice (28/10 and 30/10), which means cross-references between documents must be checked. | Reduced to 44 FR and 24 NFR following the lecturer's advice, with mapping tables between old and new IDs in each file. |
| L9 | **Limited time and parallel work** with other courses, in a group of five people writing the same document. | Risk of inconsistencies between sections written by different people. | Each person writes their section in separate `.md` files (PT and EN) in a shared repository. A single integrator moves everything into Word and checks consistency. |
| L10 | **Public market sources only.** The competitor and market analyses used only public information (websites, demos, articles). It was not possible to try the solutions from the inside or to get market figures for AMAPs in Portugal. | The proposed differentiation and market potential are estimates. | Needs and differentiations were linked to validation questions for the stakeholders (technique 4). |

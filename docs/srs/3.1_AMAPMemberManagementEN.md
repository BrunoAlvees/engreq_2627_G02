# 3.1 AMAP and member management

**Owner:** Bruno Alves · **Updated on:** 12/11/2026 · **Status:** in the report
**Sources:** [RF01_GestaoAMAPMembrosEN.md](../requisitos/RF01_GestaoAMAPMembrosEN.md) (requirements, sources and status) · acceptance criteria in [12.4](12.4_AcceptanceCriteriaEN.md)

## 3.1.1 Description
This feature lets users create and configure independent AMAPs, each with one or several producers and its own operating rules, and manages each person's path in the AMAP: the co-producer's membership request, waiting list and trial deliveries; the producer's application and departure; registration, personal data, consents and roles; and an AMAP leaving the platform. Today, joining is done by contacting the main producer directly, data arrives through Google Forms and there is no record of each member's status.

**Priority: High** (6 of its 10 requirements are Must).

## 3.1.2 Stimulus/Response Sequences

*[Figure: `docs/requisitos/diagramas/casos_uso/CasosUsoRF01EN.png`]*
*Figure 1 – Use cases of feature 3.1 (AMAP and member management)*

*Table – Stimulus/response sequences of feature 3.1*

| User action (stimulus) | System response |
|---|---|
| A person registers a new AMAP and submits the requested documents | Notifies the FAIR administrator, who checks the documents and authorises the AMAP |
| The prospective co-producer opens the AMAP's public page or searches the portal | Shows the presentation, the producers and whether there are places |
| The prospective co-producer asks to join | Records the request and notifies whoever handles it; if there are no places, adds them to the waiting list |
| AMAP management approves the request | Sets the person as "on trial" and, after the trial deliveries, invites them to subscribe to the cycle |
| A producer applies to an AMAP, or accepts an invitation from management, and submits their details and certificates | Notifies AMAP management, who usually records a farm visit and then approves or rejects the application; if approved, sets the producer as active or, if there is no capacity, adds them to the waiting list |
| A co-producer asks to leave in the middle of the subscription period | Records the withdrawal request; if management accepts it, refunds the remaining deliveries and notifies the producers to stop delivering |
| The co-producer asks for their data to be deleted | Records the request and tracks the response deadline |

### Use case specifications
The two main use cases are detailed below.

*Table – Use case: Request to join (FR-03)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Request to join |
| **Description** | A prospective co-producer asks to join an AMAP. |
| **Actors** | Prospective co-producer (primary), AMAP management |
| **Preconditions** | 1. The AMAP exists and its public page is visible. |
| **Main flow** | 1. The person opens the AMAP's public page and selects "I want to join".<br>2. The system shows the joining form and the AMAP's Terms.<br>3. The person fills in name, email and phone and accepts the Terms.<br>4. The system records the request and notifies AMAP management.<br>5. Management approves the request.<br>6. The system sets the person as "on trial" and schedules the trial deliveries. |
| **Alternative flows** | A1 (step 4): there are no places, so the system adds the person to the waiting list and notifies them when a place opens. |
| **Exceptions** | 1. Management rejects the request: the system notifies the person.<br>2. The person does not accept the Terms: the request is not submitted. |
| **Postconditions** | 1. The person is "on trial" or on the waiting list. |

*Table – Use case: Apply to an AMAP (FR-08)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Apply to an AMAP |
| **Description** | A producer applies to supply an AMAP, on their own initiative or invited by AMAP management. |
| **Actors** | Producer (primary), AMAP management |
| **Preconditions** | 1. The AMAP exists and accepts new producers. |
| **Main flow** | 1. The producer selects "Apply" on the AMAP's page.<br>2. The system asks for the application details: name, location, main products, farming method (organic or not) and supporting documents (e.g. organic certificate or other certifications).<br>3. The producer fills in the details and submits the application.<br>4. The system records the application and notifies AMAP management.<br>5. AMAP members visit the farm and record the visit (date, who went, notes).<br>6. Management approves the application.<br>7. The system sets the producer as active in the AMAP.<br>8. The producer states the dates they can deliver and what they can deliver. |
| **Alternative flows** | A1 (step 1): management invites the producer; the producer opens the invitation and continues from step 2.<br>A2 (step 5): the AMAP decides without a visit; the flow continues at step 6.<br>A3 (step 7): the AMAP has reached its maximum capacity; the system adds the producer to the waiting list and notifies them when a place opens. |
| **Exceptions** | 1. Management rejects the application: the system notifies the producer.<br>2. Required fields are missing: the system asks the producer to complete them. |
| **Postconditions** | 1. The producer is active or on the waiting list and their documents are visible on their public profile, or the application is rejected. |

## 3.1.3 Functional Requirements

*Table – Functional requirements of feature 3.1 (M = Must, S = Should, C = Could)*

| ID | Requirement | Actor | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall allow creating and configuring independent AMAPs, with one or several producers and their own operating rules. | AMAP management, FAIR administrator | M |
| FR-02 | The system shall provide a public page for each AMAP and a portal with all AMAPs on the platform, from which a prospective co-producer can request to join. | Prospective co-producer | S |
| FR-03 | The system shall manage a prospective co-producer's joining process, from the request until they become an active member, including the waiting list and trial deliveries. | Prospective co-producer, AMAP management | M |
| FR-04 | The system shall allow registering co-producers and give each producer a public profile showing how they produce. | Co-producer, Producer | M |
| FR-05 | The system shall collect members' acceptance of the terms and their consents, and allow them to exercise their personal data rights (GDPR). | Co-producer, AMAP management | M |
| FR-06 | The system shall allow each AMAP's management to assign roles within the AMAP, with different permissions, and one person may have several roles. | AMAP management, FAIR administrator | M |
| FR-07 | The system shall allow a co-producer to belong to several AMAPs and record their departure in the middle of a subscription. | Co-producer, AMAP management | S |
| FR-08 | The system shall manage a new producer joining an AMAP, from the application until they state what they can deliver and when. | Producer, AMAP management | M |
| FR-09 | The system shall manage a producer leaving an AMAP, ensuring that deliveries already paid for by co-producers are either made or compensated. | Producer, AMAP management | S |
| FR-10 | The system shall allow an AMAP to stop using the platform, exporting its data before it is anonymised. | AMAP management, FAIR administrator | C |

*Acceptance criteria for each requirement are in Appendix 12.4.*

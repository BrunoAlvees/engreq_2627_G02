# 3.2 Catalogue and offers

**Owner:** Bruno Alves · **Updated on:** 12/11/2026 · **Status:** draft for the report
**Sources:** [RF02_CatalogoOfertasEN.md](../requisitos/RF02_CatalogoOfertasEN.md) (requirements, sources and status) · acceptance criteria in [12.4](12.4_AcceptanceCriteriaEN.md)

## 3.2.1 Description
This feature lets each producer maintain their catalogue (baskets, extra products and one-off items) and set, for each product, its variants, price type, frequency and capacity. Today the catalogue lives in a Google Form: many options end up as free text and some products exist but are not listed (e.g. dried mushrooms).

**Priority: High** (4 of its 6 requirements are Must).

## 3.2.2 Stimulus/Response Sequences

*[Figure: `docs/requisitos/diagramas/casos_uso/CasosUsoRF02EN.png`]*
*Figure 2 – Use cases of feature 3.2 (Catalogue and offers)*

*Table – Stimulus/response sequences of feature 3.2*

| User action (stimulus) | System response |
|---|---|
| The producer creates a product and sets its type, price and frequency | Adds the product to the producer's catalogue, available for the next cycle |
| The producer reuses the previous cycle's catalogue | Copies the products so the producer only adjusts prices and dates |
| The producer states how many co-producers they can supply for a product | Accepts subscriptions until that number, or the AMAP's maximum capacity, is reached |
| A co-producer tries to subscribe to a product whose capacity has run out | Offers to add them to that product's waiting list and notifies them when a place opens |
| The co-producer opens a product | Shows its details sheet: description, certification, storage, origin and price with VAT |
| The producer publishes a one-off offer (e.g. a surplus) with an available quantity | Notifies the co-producers and accepts orders until the quantity runs out, then closes the offer |

### Use case specifications
The two main use cases are detailed below.

*Table – Use case: Manage catalogue (FR-11 to FR-14)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Manage catalogue |
| **Description** | A producer creates or updates the products they offer to an AMAP for the next cycle. |
| **Actors** | Producer (primary) |
| **Preconditions** | 1. The producer is active in the AMAP. |
| **Main flow** | 1. The producer opens their catalogue.<br>2. The producer creates a product and chooses its type: basket, extra or one-off.<br>3. The producer fills in the name, description, variants (e.g. size, price level) and price type (fixed fee, per unit or per kg).<br>4. The producer sets the frequency (e.g. fortnightly, monthly, only in certain months).<br>5. The producer states how many co-producers they can supply for that product.<br>6. The system validates the data and adds the product to the catalogue for the next cycle. |
| **Alternative flows** | A1 (step 2): the producer reuses the previous cycle's catalogue; the system copies the products and the producer only adjusts prices and dates.<br>A2 (step 2): the producer edits or deactivates an existing product. |
| **Exceptions** | 1. Required fields are missing (e.g. price): the system asks the producer to complete them.<br>2. The capacity stated is higher than the AMAP's maximum capacity: the system warns the producer and uses the AMAP's limit. |
| **Postconditions** | 1. The product is in the producer's catalogue and visible to co-producers when the cycle opens. |

*Table – Use case: Publish one-off offer (FR-16)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Publish one-off offer |
| **Description** | A producer offers a surplus or a new product outside the subscriptions, and co-producers order it. |
| **Actors** | Producer (primary), Co-producer |
| **Preconditions** | 1. The producer is active in the AMAP. |
| **Main flow** | 1. The producer creates a one-off offer with the product, price and available quantity.<br>2. The producer optionally limits it to a maximum number of co-producers or a maximum quantity per person.<br>3. The system publishes the offer and notifies the AMAP's co-producers.<br>4. A co-producer orders a quantity.<br>5. The system records the order and deducts it from the available quantity.<br>6. When the quantity runs out, the system closes the offer. |
| **Alternative flows** | A1 (any step): the producer changes the available quantity; the system updates it.<br>A2 (step 6): the order deadline ends before the quantity runs out; the system closes the offer. |
| **Exceptions** | 1. The co-producer orders more than is available or than the limit per person: the system rejects the order and shows the maximum allowed. |
| **Postconditions** | 1. The orders are recorded and added to each co-producer's next delivery and payment. |

## 3.2.3 Functional Requirements

*Table – Functional requirements of feature 3.2 (M = Must, S = Should, C = Could)*

| ID | Requirement | Actor | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-11 | The system shall allow the producer to manage their catalogue of baskets, extra products and one-off items, without a closed list of products. | Producer | M |
| FR-12 | The system shall allow the producer to offer several basket types and variants of each product. | Producer | M |
| FR-13 | The system shall allow setting each product's price type and frequency. | Producer | M |
| FR-14 | The system shall allow setting a maximum capacity per product and per AMAP and manage a waiting list when it is reached. | Producer | M |
| FR-15 | The system shall show the co-producer each product's details sheet. | Co-producer | S |
| FR-16 | The system shall allow the producer to publish one-off offers (e.g. surpluses or new products), which co-producers can order outside their subscription. | Producer, Co-producer | S |

*Acceptance criteria for each requirement are in Appendix 12.4.*

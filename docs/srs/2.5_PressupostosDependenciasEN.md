# 2.5 Assumptions and dependencies

**Owner:** Liane Duarte · **Updated on:** 04/11/2026 · **Status:** draft, to be completed once the requirements and use cases are final
**Sources:** source codes in [requirements/README.md](../requisitos/README.md), [open questions](../requisitos/PerguntasEmAbertoEN.md) (PA-nn), [doubts about current processes](../tecnicas/DuvidasProcessosAtuaisEN.md) (DV-nn), [2.4](2.4_RestricoesConcecaoImplementacaoEN.md)

This section lists the factors the requirements depend on but the project does not control.
- An **assumption (AS)** is something the group takes as true without having confirmed it with the stakeholders.
- A **dependency (DE)** is an external service, component, decision or rule the system needs.

If one of these turns out to be false or changes, the requirements in the "Impact if it fails" column must be reviewed. **Known constraints** (e.g. the system does not issue invoices, NFR-18) belong in section 2.4, not here.

Status: **To validate** (still to confirm with the client or a stakeholder) · **Confirmed** (becomes a fact or a requirement and leaves this list) · **False** (review the affected requirements).

## 2.5.1 Assumptions

### Users and adoption

| ID | Assumption | Impact if it fails | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS-01 | Producers, co-producers and AMAP management have a **smartphone or computer with internet access** and **basic digital literacy**. They already use Google Forms, WhatsApp, email and Excel today. | An assisted mode would be needed, with the producer or management entering data on behalf of people who do not use the system. Affects NFR-01, NFR-02, FR-18, FR-27 and FR-28. | E-2 §2, E-3, D-A | To validate |
| AS-02 | Each AMAP has **a person, committee or working group** available to configure the AMAP, approve memberships and run the cycle on the platform. | Without management, the tasks fall on the main producer, who is already overloaded today. Affects FR-01, FR-03, FR-06 and FR-17. | E-1 (v2 §31, §33), D-D | To validate |
| AS-03 | Producers are willing to **replace their Excel sheets and WhatsApp messages** with the platform, at least for orders, deliveries and payments. | With low adoption the product loses value. ## 2.5.2 Dependencies

### Third-party services and components

| ID | Dependency | Impact if it fails or changes | Requirements | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DE-01 | A **licensed payment provider** (e.g. MB Way, through an aggregator) able to **split one payment among several producers** and report the status of each payment. | Without it, centralised collection is not possible (see AS-09). The provider's fees affect the revenue model. | NFR-17, NFR-19, FR-31 to FR-33 | To validate (C6) |
| DE-02 | A reliable **email delivery service** for notifications, reminders and payment requests. | Reminders and notices stop arriving and another channel is needed. | NFR-15, FR-35, FR-36, FR-31 | To validate |
| DE-03 | **Browser push notifications** (Web Push). On iPhone they only work with the PWA installed on the home screen. | iPhone users who do not install the PWA only get email. | NFR-01, NFR-15, FR-38 | To validate |
| DE-04 | *(Could)* The official **WhatsApp Business API** for individual messages. It has per-message costs and Meta's rules. | If the API is unavailable or too expensive, only email and push remain. | NFR-15 | To validate |
| DE-05 | **Cloud hosting** with daily backups and, preferably, servers in the EU (GDPR). | Availability and data recovery depend on the provider. | NFR-08, NFR-11 | To validate |

## 2.5.3 Keeping this list up to date

- **While working:** whenever a requirement, use case or precondition depends on something not confirmed, add a row here with the next free number. IDs are never reused.
- **After the next client session:** confirmed assumptions are set to "Confirmed" (with the source) and removed from the final SRS or turned into requirements. False ones trigger a review of the listed requirements.
- **Before moving to Word:** check that the FR/NFR IDs cited still exist, since the requirements were renumbered on 28/10 and 30/10.

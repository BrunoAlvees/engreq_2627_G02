# 2.5 Assumptions and dependencies

**Owner:** Liane Duarte · **Updated on:** 11/11/2026 · **Status:** updated with the session of 10/11 (E-8), the last one with the client before delivery
**Sources:** source codes in [requirements/README.md](../requisitos/README.md), [open questions](../requisitos/PerguntasEmAbertoEN.md) (PA-nn), [doubts about current processes](../tecnicas/DuvidasProcessosAtuaisEN.md) (DV-nn), [2.4](2.4_RestricoesConcecaoImplementacaoEN.md), [interview of 10/11](../interviews/10-11-2026/Entrevista10_11EN.md)

This section lists the factors the requirements depend on but the project does not control.
- An **assumption (AS)** is something the group takes as true without having confirmed it with the stakeholders.
- A **dependency (DE)** is an external service, component, decision or rule the system needs.

If one of these turns out to be false or changes, the requirements in the "Impact if it fails" column must be reviewed. **Known constraints** (e.g. the system does not issue invoices, NFR-18) belong in section 2.4, not here.

The session of 10/11 (E-8) was the last chance to talk to the client before the SRS was delivered. Assumptions that could not be confirmed remain a **group decision**: the system follows the assumption and, where possible, makes the rule configurable per AMAP, so the decision can be corrected without changing the software. The requirements that depend on them have the status "Proposed", not "Validated". In a future iteration with the client, the "Assumed" items and the questions that were not asked (section 4 of the interview of 10/11) should be the starting point.

Status: **Assumed** (not confirmed; the group follows the assumption) · **Confirmed** (confirmed by the client or a stakeholder, with the source) · **Partly confirmed** (states what is missing) · **Partly false** (part of it turned out false; the status says which part) · **False** (the affected requirements were revised).

Dependency status: **Required** or **Optional** (services) · **Accepted by the client** · **Assumed** · **Resolved** or **Partly resolved** (decisions) · **In force** (legislation).

## 2.5.1 Assumptions

### Users and adoption

| ID | Assumption | Impact if it fails | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS-01 | Producers, co-producers and AMAP management have a **smartphone or computer with internet access** and **basic digital literacy**. They already use Google Forms, WhatsApp, email and Excel today. | An assisted mode would be needed, with the producer or management entering data on behalf of people who do not use the system. Affects NFR-01, NFR-02, FR-18, FR-27 and FR-28. | E-2 §2, E-3, D-A | Assumed |
| AS-02 | Each AMAP has **a person, committee or working group** available to configure the AMAP, approve memberships and run the cycle on the platform. | Without management, the tasks fall on the main producer, who is already overloaded today. Affects FR-01, FR-03, FR-06 and FR-17. | E-1 (v2 §31, §33), D-D, E-8 | Confirmed (E-8): management assesses applications and accepts members. In an AMAP started by a producer, that producer holds both the manager and producer roles. |
| AS-03 | Producers are willing to **replace their Excel sheets and WhatsApp messages** with the platform, at least for orders, deliveries and payments. | With low adoption the product loses value. Data import and export (NFR-16, FR-42) and keeping WhatsApp as a channel (NFR-15) would become more important. | E-2 §2, §5, DV-09, N19, E-8 | Assumed. ## 2.5.2 Dependencies

### Third-party services and components

| ID | Dependency | Impact if it fails or changes | Requirements | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DE-01 | A **licensed payment provider** (e.g. Stripe, or MB Way through an aggregator) able to **split one payment among several producers** and report the status of each payment. | Without it, centralised collection is not possible (see AS-09). The provider's fees are a producer cost (E-8). | NFR-17, NFR-19, FR-31 to FR-33 | Accepted by the client (E-8); provider to be chosen |
| DE-02 | A reliable **email delivery service** for notifications, reminders and payment requests. | Reminders and notices stop arriving and another channel is needed. | NFR-15, FR-35, FR-36, FR-31 | Required |
| DE-03 | **Browser push notifications** (Web Push). On iPhone they only work with the PWA installed on the home screen. | iPhone users who do not install the PWA only get email. | NFR-01, NFR-15, FR-38 | Required |
| DE-04 | *(Could)* The official **WhatsApp Business API** for individual messages. It has per-message costs and Meta's rules. | If the API is unavailable or too expensive, only email and push remain. | NFR-15 | Optional |
| DE-05 | **Cloud hosting** with daily backups and, preferably, servers in the EU (GDPR). | Availability and data recovery depend on the provider. | NFR-08, NFR-11 | Required |

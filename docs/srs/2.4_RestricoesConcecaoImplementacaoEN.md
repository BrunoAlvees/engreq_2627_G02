# 2.4 Design and implementation constraints

**Owner:** Bruno Alves · **Updated on:** 21/10/2026 · **Status:** draft for review
**Sources:** [RNF_AtributosQualidadeEN.md](../requisitos/RNF_AtributosQualidadeEN.md) §7 and source codes in [requisitos/README.md](../requisitos/README.md)

This section describes the factors that **limit the options** of whoever designs and develops FAIR-AMAP: regulation, AMAP principles, FAIR's business model, architecture and technology. Non-functional requirements that also act as constraints are only referenced here; their detail is in sections 6, 7 and 8.

## 2.4.1 Regulatory constraints

| ID | Constraint | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-18 | The system **does not issue invoices**: invoicing is **out of scope**. Each producer keeps invoicing in their own software. | E-6 (the client confirmed invoices are out of scope); Technique 2 results §5, C4 | Validated |
| NFR-19 | The system **does not hold co-producers' money directly**, because that would be a payment service subject to PSD2. Collection is **centralised**: the co-producer makes a single payment, which is split among producers through a **licensed payment provider**, and each producer receives a summary of who paid and how much. *(Option chosen by the group on 21/10; the current model, where each co-producer pays each producer directly, was set aside.)* | Group proposal (based on E-5), PSD2, E-1, E-2 §13 | Proposed |
| — | **Data protection (GDPR).** Processing personal data constrains the design: authentication and role-based access (NFR-04), encrypted communications (NFR-05), allergies and intolerances treated as **health data** with explicit consent (NFR-06) and never printed on labels (NFR-06), controller and processor roles (NFR-08) and retention periods (NFR-08). | GDPR, D-B §3.1 | See section 7 |

## 2.4.2 AMAP principles and business model

| ID | Constraint | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-20 | The solution respects the **AMAP Charter of Principles**: it is an organisation tool and **must not act as a commercial intermediary** between producer and co-producer. Direct contact between them always remains visible. | D-E (Charter of Principles: "without intervention of intermediary agents"), D-C §4.1, E-2, E-3 §5, CA; "organisation tool" is the group's interpretation (technique 2, results §5); principle 2 of the 14/10 brainstorm | To validate (C6) |
| NFR-21 | FAIR charges a **minimum fee per producer** plus a **percentage of the payment volume** that goes through the platform. The amounts are set by FAIR. | Group proposal (based on E-1, E-5) | Proposed |

> **Note (NFR-21):** the model follows the FAIR client's suggestion (minimum fee that grows with volume, E-1) and centralised collection (NFR-19, E-5). It is only viable at **scale** (many AMAPs on the same platform, NFR-22, NFR-13). The competitor analysis and the Charter of Principles (CA, D-E) favoured a model without a per-transaction commission; the group chose volume because FAIR neither buys nor resells products and the price is still set by the producer.

**Scope decision C6 (summary):** the initial proposal was an **organisation tool** that **neither handles money nor issues invoices** (NFR-18, 02, 03). After the lecturer's remark about **centralised collection** (E-5), the group chose (21/10) to **centralise collection through a licensed provider** (NFR-19; NFR-17). The system still **does not issue invoices** and **does not hold the money**. The client still has to confirm, and compatibility with the Charter of Principles (NFR-20) must be checked. Affected requirements are marked "To validate (C6)".

## 2.4.3 Architecture and technology

| ID | Constraint | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-22 | The solution is a **platform shared by several AMAPs** (multi-AMAP), with each AMAP's data kept separate. A person may be a member of more than one AMAP. | CA, E-2 §1, FR-07 | Proposed |
| NFR-23 *(new)* | The solution must be **maintained by FAIR** over time (fixes, security updates and evolution). The client mentioned that software built by amateurs for CSAs stops being maintained. | E-1 (23/09 session) | Proposed |
| NFR-24 *(new)* | Differences **between AMAPs and between producers in the same AMAP** (cycle length, delivery dates and mode, change deadlines, payment timing, absence rules) are handled through **configuration**, without specific development for each AMAP or producer. | Principle 1 of the 14/10 brainstorm, E-2 §1, §11, E-3 §2–§4, §8 | Proposed |
| — | **Web application** for mobile and desktop, **with no mandatory installation**; it can be installed on the home screen as a **PWA**, without going through app stores, and the app itself explains how (NFR-01). **Push** notifications in addition to email (NFR-15). | E-3, group decision of 21/10 | See sections 6 and 7 |
| — | **Language:** interface in **European Portuguese**, ready for other languages; euros with VAT, dd/mm/yyyy dates, Portuguese time zone (NFR-13, 02). | CA, D-A | See section 8 |
| — | **Mandatory interfaces:** **email** (NFR-15) and phone **push** notifications (NFR-15), and spreadsheet export (NFR-16) for those who use Excel today. | D-A, E-2 §2, group decision of 21/10 | See section 6 |

## 2.4.4 Open points
- **C6:** does the client confirm centralised collection through a licensed provider? Is it compatible with the AMAP Charter of Principles? (affects NFR-18, 02, 03, NFR-17 and RF05)
- **E-5** was overheard in a conversation between the lecturer and another group and was not recorded: confirm directly with the lecturer.
- **DV-25:** confirm with FAIR the minimum fee and percentage amounts (affects NFR-21).
- **NFR-23** and **NFR-24** are new (21/10) and already in the [NFR](../requisitos/RNF_AtributosQualidadeEN.md) table §7; the group still has to agree.

# 2.4 Design and implementation constraints

**Owner:** Bruno Alves · **Updated on:** 21/10/2026 · **Status:** draft for review
**Sources:** [RNF_AtributosQualidadeEN.md](../requisitos/RNF_AtributosQualidadeEN.md) §7 and source codes in [requisitos/README.md](../requisitos/README.md)

This section describes the factors that **limit the options** of whoever designs and develops FAIR-AMAP: regulation, AMAP principles, FAIR's business model, architecture and technology. Non-functional requirements that also act as constraints are only referenced here; their detail is in sections 6, 7 and 8.

## 2.4.1 Regulatory constraints

| ID | Constraint | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| RNF-RES-01 | The system **does not issue invoices**. Issuing invoices in Portugal requires software certified by the Tax Authority (AT). Each producer keeps invoicing in their own software; the system may only record the invoice number. | Technique 2 results §5, C4 | To validate (C6) |
| RNF-RES-02 | The system **does not hold co-producers' money directly**, because that would be a payment service subject to PSD2. Two options remain open: **(A)** each co-producer pays each producer directly (current model, e.g. MB Way, bank transfer) and the system records payments and calculates amounts; **(B)** **centralised collection**: the co-producer makes a single payment, which is split among producers through a **licensed payment provider**, and each producer receives a summary of who paid and how much. | D-C §4.3, PSD2, E-3 (model A); E-1, E-2 §13, E-5 (model B) | To validate (C6) |
| — | **Data protection (GDPR).** Processing personal data constrains the design: authentication and role-based access (RNF-SEG-01), encrypted communications (RNF-SEG-02), allergies and intolerances treated as **health data** with explicit consent (RNF-SEG-03) and never printed on labels (RNF-SEG-07), controller and processor roles (RNF-SEG-05) and retention periods (RNF-SEG-06). | GDPR, D-B §3.1 | See section 7 |

## 2.4.2 AMAP principles and business model

| ID | Constraint | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| RNF-RES-03 | The solution respects the **AMAP Charter of Principles**: it is an organisation tool and **must not act as a commercial intermediary** between producer and co-producer. Direct contact between them always remains visible. | D-C §4.1, D-E, CA, principle 2 of the 14/10 brainstorm | To validate (C6) |
| RNF-RES-04 | FAIR's pricing model **must not depend on a commission per basket or per transaction**. | CA, D-E | To validate (DV-25) ⚠️ |

> ⚠️ **Conflict between sources (RNF-RES-04):** in the 23/09 session, the FAIR client suggested a **minimum fee per producer that grows with transaction volume** (E-1). In a class during the week of 12/10, the lecturer again described a model where **subscription money is collected in one place and then distributed among producers** (E-5), which allows charging on volume. The competitor analysis and the AMAP documents point to a model **without a per-transaction commission**, consistent with AMAP ethics. The decision belongs to the client (DV-25). Until then, the constraint stays "To validate".

**Scope decision C6 (summary):** the initial proposal was an **organisation tool** that **neither handles money nor issues invoices** (RNF-RES-01, 02, 03). The lecturer's remark about **centralised collection** (E-5) opens an alternative: the system still **does not issue invoices** and **does not hold the money**, but may centralise collection through a licensed provider (RNF-RES-02, model B; RNF-INT-04). The choice between models A and B depends on the client and on compatibility with the Charter of Principles (RNF-RES-03). Affected requirements are marked "To validate (C6)".

## 2.4.3 Architecture and technology

| ID | Constraint | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| RNF-RES-05 | The solution is a **platform shared by several AMAPs** (multi-AMAP), with each AMAP's data kept separate. A person may be a member of more than one AMAP. | CA, E-2 §1, RF-MEM-20 | Proposed |
| RNF-RES-06 *(new)* | The solution must be **maintained by FAIR** over time (fixes, security updates and evolution). The client mentioned that software built by amateurs for CSAs stops being maintained. | E-1 (23/09 session) | To validate |
| RNF-RES-07 *(new)* | Differences between AMAPs (cycle length, delivery days, absence rules, each producer's rules) are handled through **configuration**, without specific development for each AMAP. | Principle 1 of the 14/10 brainstorm, E-2 §1, §11 | Proposed |
| — | **Web access** on mobile and desktop, **with nothing to install** for co-producer functions (RNF-USA-01). | E-3, Hyp. | See section 7 |
| — | **Language:** interface in **European Portuguese**, ready for other languages; euros with VAT, dd/mm/yyyy dates, Portuguese time zone (RNF-I18N-01, 02). | CA, D-A | See section 8 |
| — | **Mandatory interfaces:** **email** notifications (RNF-INT-01) and spreadsheet export (RNF-INT-02) for those who use Excel today. | D-A, E-2 §2 | See section 6 |

## 2.4.4 Open points
- **C6:** direct payments to each producer (model A) or centralised collection through a licensed provider (model B)? Is centralised collection compatible with the AMAP Charter of Principles? (affects RNF-RES-01, 02, 03, RNF-INT-04 and RF05)
- **E-5** was overheard in a conversation between the lecturer and another group and was not recorded: confirm directly with the lecturer.
- **DV-25:** which pricing model does FAIR intend? (affects RNF-RES-04)
- **RNF-RES-06** and **RNF-RES-07** are new (21/10) and already in the [NFR](../requisitos/RNF_AtributosQualidadeEN.md) table §7; the group still has to agree.

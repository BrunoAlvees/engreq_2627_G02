# 10.2 Stakeholders and RACI matrix

**Owner:** Leonor Marinho · **Updated on:** 04/11/2026 · **Status:** draft for review
**Sources:** [interviews/](../interviews/), [requisitos/README.md](../requisitos/README.md) (actors), [document analysis results](../tecnicas/ResultadosAnaliseDocumentalEN.md), [SRS work split](../SRSWorkSplitEN.md)

The lecturer asked in the first session that stakeholders be identified. This section lists **who has an interest in FAIR-AMAP**, how much each can influence it, whether the group already heard them, and **who does what** in the main AMAP processes (RACI). Influence and interest levels are the group's **judgement**, not stakeholder statements.

## 10.2.2 RACI matrix: AMAP processes

**R** = Responsible (does it) · **A** = Accountable (decides, one per row) · **C** = Consulted · **I** = Informed.
This is the group's **proposal** from the interviews and the documents; the highlighted rows depend on open questions and must be **validated with the stakeholders** (especially AMAP management).

| Process | Co-producer | Producer | AMAP management | FAIR admin | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Create and configure an AMAP on the platform | I | C | C | **A/R** | FR-01 *(to validate)* |
| Define delivery dates and calendar of the cycle | I | **A/R** | C | I | E-3 §3, FR-24 |
| Publish catalogue and offers | I | **A/R** | C | I | E-1 §3.5, FR-11 |
| Admit a new co-producer / waiting list | C | I | **A/R** | I | E-1 §3.1, FR-03 |
| Admit a new producer to an AMAP | C | C | **A/R** | I | E-7, FR-08 *(to validate)* |
| Subscribe to a cycle | **A/R** | I | I | – | FR-18 |
| Publish basket contents before delivery | I | **A/R** | I | – | E-2 §4, FR-25 |
| Report absence and choose where the basket goes | **A/R** | I | I | – | E-2 §9, FR-27 |
| Confirm collection of the basket | **A/R** | I | I | – | E-3 §9, FR-28 *(to validate)* |
| Record missing items and compensations | I | **A/R** | I | – | E-3 §7, FR-26 |
| Pay the amount due | **A/R** | I | I | I | FR-32 *(C6)* |
| Track payments and late payments | I | I | **A/R** | I | E-1 §3.2, FR-33 |
| Issue invoices *(outside the system)* | I | **A/R** | – | – | E-6, NFR-18 |
| Send notices to all members | I | – | **A/R** | – | FR-36 |
| Keep rules and documents up to date | I | C | **A/R** | – | E-3 §12, FR-39 |
| Maintain and evolve the platform | I | I | I | **A/R** | NFR-23 |

> **Why the matrix matters for the requirements:** each "R" is a user who needs a feature (and permissions, NFR-04); each "A" says who can approve or configure it. A row with no clear "A" in the stakeholder answers is a good question for the next session.
